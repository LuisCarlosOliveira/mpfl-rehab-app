# UI redesign survey log

Date: 2026-11-09. Tool: Playwright browser. Catalog: https://designeer.xyz. Screenshots are in the scratchpad folder, not in the repo.

Method. Each entry page was opened. The main video (file 1.mp4) was paused at half of its length. A screenshot of the video was saved. READ means a fact came from page text, video metadata, CSS or a live site. SEEN means a fact comes from the eye only. Catalog tags and captions are READ.

Note on the catalog. Most entries are motion studies, not full app screens. Only a few have a live demo. No entry was walled. Entry 155 is a catalog page and not a design. Rows with an L id are live sites that entries link to.

Hard limits decided kept or dropped. They are: no green or red state colours, no score, no verdict words, no goals or streaks, no celebration, in-range shows nothing.

## Shortlist of 10

1. 156: Error and empty states. Borrow the card with a "send fails" state. Show a plain failure line and one retry button. No red.
2. 072: Setup camera. Borrow the box that acts out each step. Use neutral grey-blue marks, not green.
3. 098: Progress. Borrow the card with a big number, a Day/Week toggle and bars. Use one colour. Live demo at https://bencho.dev/.
4. 117: Progress. Borrow the legend list with a toggle. Use neutral tones only. Live library at https://uiarc.dev/.
5. 112: Summary. Borrow the ruler slider with a value label for the pain input.
6. 084: Session. Borrow the dot-matrix text style for the knee angle number. It reads well from far away.
7. 079: Session. Borrow the grow and shrink motion for the cue banner.
8. 088: Red flag. Borrow the pull gesture to confirm. It stops a tap by mistake.
9. 064: Home. Borrow the bottom tab bar and the file card layout.
10. L4 (https://ocelli.app/): Session. Borrow the top bar with Pause and Stop and the time.
