## What the evidence shows
Sources are from the project files `research/rehab-rules.md`, `research/clinical-update.md`, `research/citation-audit.md` and `research/source-audit.md`. I did not open the original papers in this run.

Practice (a brace is common):
- S10 (Vidra 2026, 35 isolated MPFLR protocols): locked brace 19 (54%). Brace 0 to 6 weeks 22 (63%).
- R1: "25 (93%) recommended immediate postoperative bracing".
- R3: "Thirty protocols (97%) recommended immediate postoperative knee bracing".
- S9: "Six of seven studies (85.7%) braced patients for up to 6 weeks".
- Protocols differ. P1 (HSS) locks the brace at 0 degrees for weeks 0 to 6. P4 takes it off after 6 weeks. P5 and P8 lock it for 4 to 6 weeks. P9 uses no brace.

Why this is not proof of benefit:
- N1 (Delphi): "no consensus on postoperative immobilization or weightbearing".
- S8 (Schaver 2024): reoperation in 20 knees (12.1%) braced versus 0 (0%) unbraced. This is a retrospective study and it favors no brace. Median time to straight leg raise without lag was 44 days braced and 41 days unbraced.
- S7: in 123 knees, accelerated rehab (no brace, no weight limit) did not raise redislocation. All 3 recurrent dislocations (2.4%) were in the restrictive group.
- N3 (Hysing-Dahl and Inderhaug 2024, editorial): suggests brace-free rehab. The authors call the evidence "sparse".
- No new trial compares brace with no brace after isolated MPFLR (`clinical-update.md`, section 2.1).
- N2 (ESSKA 2025) says no brace is better than a brace after a first dislocation (Grade B). This is not post-operative, so it does not decide the question.

## Conclusion
- "Most protocols use a brace in the first weeks" is true.
- "A brace for 6 weeks is evidence-based" is not supported. No trial shows that 6 weeks of bracing helps. The retrospective data do not favor bracing. Protocols range from no brace to 6 weeks.
- Mark: REVIEW (practice), with no consensus. Do not mark it VERIFIED.
- App rule from the project research: brace use is per surgeon. The app must not set a default brace duration.
- Combined surgery (for example tibial tubercle osteotomy) is a different case. S10 reports a locked brace longer than 6 weeks in 67% of those protocols.
