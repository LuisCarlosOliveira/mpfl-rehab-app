## Result: the claim is correct for the abstract. Full text not opened.

Claim in research/clinical.md (section 4, line 125): S18 gives 11.2 MPa for the forward step-up at 45 degrees of knee flexion.

Check: I read the Europe PMC record of PMID 21289449 (REST API). The record matches the citation: Chinkulprasert C et al., "Patellofemoral joint forces and stress during forward step-up, lateral step-up, and forward step-down exercises", J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 2011, 41(4), pages 241-248, DOI 10.2519/jospt.2011.3408.

The abstract says peak patellofemoral joint stress was greater in the forward step-down (FSD, 13.8 +/- 0.4 MPa) than in the lateral step-up (LSU, 11.5 +/- 0.8 MPa, P<.001) and the forward step-up (FSU, 11.2 +/- 0.6 MPa, P = .002).

So 11.2 MPa is the forward step-up value. It is exact. The other values in the base also match: step-down 13.8 MPa and lateral step-up 11.5 MPa.

Other facts from the abstract: 20 healthy adults, repeated-measures lab study, step height set for 45 degrees of knee flexion, modelled (not measured in vivo) force and stress. Peak force was 51.1 +/- 2.7 N/kg (step-down), 44.1 +/- 3.4 (lateral step-up), 43.6 +/- 2.3 (forward step-up).

Limits:
- Access level is V-abstract. The DOI redirects to the JOSPT page, which I did not read (paywall). I did not check the angle details or the table in the full text.
- Population is BORROWED (healthy adults, not MPFL reconstruction patients). The value is a model estimate. It does not show safety for a graft.
- The knowledge base has a note that an earlier audit marked this value as unverified (correction C6) and later withdrew that note. The abstract supports the value.
