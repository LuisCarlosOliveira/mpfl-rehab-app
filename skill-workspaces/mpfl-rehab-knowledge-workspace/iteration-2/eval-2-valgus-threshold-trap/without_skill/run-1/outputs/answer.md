# Valgus angle for "bad form" in a mini squat

No angle. The app must not tell the patient that their form is bad because of valgus.

- No validated valgus threshold exists after MPFL reconstruction (research/form.md, summary). Any threshold is UNVERIFIED (clinical.md section 4).
- The cut-offs that exist come from PFP or ACL work, mostly small studies. One example is more than 10 degrees in a video method, with 30 athletes.
- A 710-athlete ACL cohort found no link between valgus and injury (Krosshaug 2016).
- A webcam gives a poor absolute angle. MediaPipe bias was about -19 degrees. The change from the start of the rep is more reliable (Asaeda 2024, form.md).
- form.md lists absolute valgus as "not reliable enough", and no "good form" label or score is allowed.

What the app can do: show a measured change against a limit that the clinician sets for that patient (MPFL_APP_PLAN.md). The wording must be neutral. Valgus is not in the MVP checks. The MVP flexion checks use the side view (form-opus.md, C1 and C2).
