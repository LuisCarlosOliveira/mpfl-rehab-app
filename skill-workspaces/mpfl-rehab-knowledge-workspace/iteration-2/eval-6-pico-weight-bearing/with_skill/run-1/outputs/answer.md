Answer for isolated adult MPFLR first. Evidence is low and sources disagree. The decision belongs to the surgeon (`research/clinical.md` section 9 item 2). Ask your surgeon.

PICO: surgery type, age group, weeks since surgery are not given. Cases:
- Isolated MPFLR, adult: S1 (Hsu 2025, V, LoE 5): usually not limited. Crutches help at first. S10 (Vidra 2026, V, 35 isolated protocols): immediate WBAT in 66 percent. S7 (retrospective, 123 knees): accelerated rehab did not raise redislocation (3 events, all in the restrictive group). Quality: low, no RCT (section 8).
- Older view: S3 (2018, 53 studies): restrictions in 90.6 percent. S2 (2022): about 80 percent of weight-bearing programmes had non-weight-bearing in week 1. These reflect older studies.
- Combined surgery (TTO, trochleoplasty, cartilage): S10 delayed or no weight-bearing in 56 percent. S2: immobilisation in week 1 in 93.8 percent. Zhang 2024 RCT (N=50, LoE 2, V-abstract, MPFLR-COMBINED): rapid weight-bearing gave better scores to 12 months, no difference at 24 months. Do not use an isolated rule for combined surgery.
- Adolescents: Mass General Brigham (2024) 25 percent with crutches, weeks 0 to 2. Banff (2019, over 5 years old) about half body weight at first.
- Delphi N1 (60 surgeons): no consensus (V-abstract). Protocols (`clinical-update.md`) mostly WBAT. Dated sources older than 5 years need a newer search.
