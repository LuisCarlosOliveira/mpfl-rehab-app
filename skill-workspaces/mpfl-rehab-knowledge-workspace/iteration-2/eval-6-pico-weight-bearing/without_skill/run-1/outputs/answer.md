Question (PICO): In patients after isolated MPFL reconstruction (P), does immediate weight-bearing as tolerated (I) compared with restricted weight-bearing (C) change outcomes such as stability, ROM and return to sport (O)?

Short answer: the evidence is low quality and does not settle it.
- No RCT compares weight-bearing schedules after isolated MPFLR (research/clinical-update.md).
- Weight-bearing as tolerated is acceptable after isolated MPFLR. Support is S1 (LoE 5), S7 (retrospective, 123 knees) and S10 (structured review). Confidence is low (research/clinical.md).
- S10: immediate WBAT in 66 percent of 35 isolated MPFLR protocols. Other reviews report restrictions in many programmes (S3 90.6 percent, S2 about 80 percent with non-weight-bearing in week 1). The sources disagree.
- N1 (Hurley 2023, Delphi, 60 surgeons) found no consensus on immobilization or weight-bearing.
- Combined surgery (TTO, trochleoplasty, cartilage work) is different. Protocols are more protective, often delayed or partial weight-bearing.
- N5 (Zhang 2024, RCT, N=50) favours rapid rehab early, with no difference at 24 months. It is not a weight-bearing trial alone.

For the app: do not pick a side. Show the range, state the low confidence, and defer to the surgeon.
