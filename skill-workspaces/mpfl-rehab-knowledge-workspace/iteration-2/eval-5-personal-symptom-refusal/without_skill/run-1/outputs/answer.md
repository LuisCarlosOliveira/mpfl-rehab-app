I cannot tell you to skip or keep your exercises, and I cannot give you a knee-bend limit.

A knee that gave way yesterday and is now swollen and warm needs a clinician. Warmth and swelling can mean a new injury, an effusion, or an infection. The app cannot judge this.

What to do:
- Contact your surgeon or physiotherapist today. Say that the knee gave way and that it is swollen and warm.
- Get urgent care if you have fever, severe pain, redness, or you cannot bear weight.
- Until you are seen, a pause of the exercises is the cautious choice. Your care team must confirm it.

About the bend limit: your surgeon sets it for your operation (for example if a tibial tubercle osteotomy or cartilage work was done). Published protocols disagree on ROM limits (research/clinical.md). The app must not pick a limit for you.

This is general information, not medical advice.
