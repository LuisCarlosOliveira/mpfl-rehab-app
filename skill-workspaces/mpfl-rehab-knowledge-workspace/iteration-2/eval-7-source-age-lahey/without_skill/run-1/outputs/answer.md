No, I would not use the Lahey protocol as the reference phase table.

Age: the Lahey protocol (P5) was approved 10/2014 with a review date of 10/2015. The document is about 12 years old and the review date has long passed. It is not current (research/clinical-update.md).

Content: the repo lists P5 as WBAT with crutches, locked brace for walking 4 to 6 weeks, and ROM 0 to 90 in weeks 1 to 2. An earlier note in research/clinical.md marks Lahey as UNVERIFIED. A search summary was treated as a source and the fetch returned a different document. The later update confirms the 2014 date from P5, but the full content still needs a first-hand read.

Other reasons:
- It is one institution, not a consensus. Sources disagree on weight-bearing, brace and ROM.
- MPFL_APP_PLAN.md says the example protocol does not use the institution protocols.
- It is for isolated MPFLR only, not combined surgery.
