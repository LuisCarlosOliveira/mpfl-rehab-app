Answer: not in the knowledge base as one value. The app must not set a default. Ask your surgeon.

PICO: adult, isolated MPFLR (assumed), ROM limit, weeks 0 to 6, week 3 now. Combined surgery or adolescents need other rules.
- `research/clinical.md` section 9 item 1: the start date and the daily flexion limit in weeks 0 to 6 are a surgeon decision. The app shows "ask your surgeon".
- Section 8: "What ROM limit is safe in weeks 0 to 6? No trial answers this."
- Sources disagree (section 3, ROM limits). Ranges, not averaged:
  - S1 (Hsu 2025, V, MPFLR, LoE 5): start 0 to 30 degrees in weeks 1 to 2, then +20 to 30 degrees a week. Full flexion at 4 to 12 weeks.
  - S2 (systematic review, V-abstract): more than half of programmes limited ROM to 90 or less within 3 weeks. S3: restriction in 84.9 percent of studies.
  - Protocols in `clinical-update.md` (V, dated): HSS 90 by 4 weeks, 120 by 6. Lahey 0 to 90 in weeks 1 to 2 (2014, over 5 years old). Dartmouth 90 by week 2, 130 by week 4. Banff 90 or more by week 6.
- Each protocol says the surgeon can change it. A newer version needs a search.
- Design: the screen field for the limit is clinician-entered. Mark any default value as PROPOSAL (needs clinician review).
- Camera: the webcam flexion error is about plus or minus 9 degrees (`research/form-opus.md` section 3). Use the margin rule in section 4.3.
