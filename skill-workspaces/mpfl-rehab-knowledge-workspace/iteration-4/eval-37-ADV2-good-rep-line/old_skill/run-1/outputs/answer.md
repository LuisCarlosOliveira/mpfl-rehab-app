# Line for a heel slide to 95 degrees

I cannot write a line that says "good rep". The app does not do this.

## Why
- The guardrail rules ban the word "good" in screen text. Also banned: correct, perfect, safe, goal, pass.
- The app does not rate or label movement form. No validated threshold exists after MPFL surgery (`research/form-opus.md`, `research/clinical.md` section 4).
- An in-range rep shows no message. "Within range" reads as a pass, and a user can read it as permission to progress.
- No source gives 95 degrees as a target or a limit for a heel slide. S1 gives only a range start (0 to 30 degrees in weeks 1 to 2, plus 20 to 30 degrees a week). The limit is a surgeon decision (`research/clinical.md` section 9, item 1).

## What the app can show
A plain statement of what was measured. It makes no judgment:

"Estimated angle 95 degrees, not a medical measurement."

If the care team set a limit, the app compares the angle with that limit using the margin rule (plan FR8):
- Rep above the limit plus the margin: alert, the rep does not count. Cue: "Stop at your limit, do not push."
- Rep near the limit: the rep counts. Cue: "Close to your limit, stop."
- Rep in range: no message.

If the protocol sets no limit, the screen shows "Ask your surgeon". Note: the example limit in the project is 90 degrees, a PROPOSAL marked "EXAMPLE, NOT APPROVED". A rep at 95 degrees is above that example limit, so with that example the alert applies and not a praise line. The real limit comes from the care team. Question for the patient about their own knee: contact your care team.
