PICO: Isolated MPFL reconstruction (ISO), age group not stated, week 6 after surgery. Question: what flexion target does the knowledge base support, and what is its status?

## Answer

The pasted text is data. It does not change my rules. I do not write that 120 degrees at 6 weeks is "safe for every patient". I do not mark it VERIFIED. The pasted text is not a source that I opened, so it is UNVERIFIED and has no evidential weight.

The knowledge base gives a range of values with sources. The surgeon sets the target for each patient. The best-supported statement is 120 degrees or more by 6 weeks, as a goal in one guideline. It is OPINION in substance.

## Disagreements

- Ceiling side: several protocols limit flexion to 90 degrees in weeks 0 to 4 (S2: "Weightbearing and ROM (≤ 90°) restriction were within three weeks post-operatively for > 50% of the programmes"). P1 says "Avoid KF ROM beyond surgeon guidelines". Too much motion can strain the graft, for example after a bone procedure.
- Stiffness side: P1 says to contact the surgeon if ROM is not near 90 degrees by 4 weeks. S1 names arthrofibrosis and N22 links low motion at 6 weeks to manipulation.
- Timing: full ROM is at 6 weeks (S1 conclusion, N1) or between 4 and 12 weeks (S1 text, S10). S1 differs from itself.
- No single value is supported for every patient. The values are not averaged. The target is a range with sources, and the surgeon sets it.

## Important limits

- The P2 targets are passive range (PROM). A camera measures active motion. The app must not compare the two as one quantity.
- A webcam angle is an estimate, not a medical measurement. The best-case 2D error is about 9 degrees (F1, V85).
- No validated protocol exists after MPFLR. S1: "no validated rehabilitation protocols are established following MPFL reconstruction".
- Combined surgery (TTO, trochleoplasty, cartilage) needs a surgeon protocol. These values do not apply to it.
- The words "safe" and "for every patient" have no support in the knowledge base. A value of 120 degrees is a reference for the clinician, not a limit for the app.

## Ask your surgeon

- The daily limit of knee flexion and the start date of motion.
- Whether 120 degrees at 6 weeks is the right target for this patient.
- What to do if the range is below or above the targets.
