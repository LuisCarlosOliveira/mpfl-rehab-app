Notes on the heel slide row:
- The old line "P1: heel slides in the AROM progression" is WRONG (audit row V37, `research/citation-audit.md`). The audit blocked it. In P1, heel slides start in phase 2 "once KF >120". S1 and P3 put them in phase 1.

**What the literature says instead**
- The only dose number for strengthening is P2 rec 26: "2 sets of 10-15 repetitions ... with progression to 3 sets" (P2, level 5, ages 8 to 25, CONSENSUS low, OPINION in substance, VERIFIED). It applies to strengthening exercises, not to a heel slide range exercise. Do not copy it into a heel slide row.
- The 2 second value in `research/ux-flow.md` ("at least 2 seconds for one rep") is a PROPOSAL of the app team. It is not evidence. If you use it, label it PROPOSAL. It needs clinician review.
- P3 gives "Ice 20 minutes, 2 hourly". This is ice, not exercise. Do not use it as a session count.
- No validated protocol exists (S1, S2). No trial compares dose after MPFLR.
