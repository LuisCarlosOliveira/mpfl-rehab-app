## What the knowledge base says about the pain stop
- No post-operative MPFLR source gives a numeric pain stop. P2 (Cincinnati Children's) gives a phase goal of pain 2 of 10 or less. S1 uses pain below 2 of 10 for return to run (`research/clinical.md` section 7, `research/rehab-rules.md` section 4).
- The 5 of 10 model is BORROWED from patellofemoral pain and tendinopathy (Thomeé via Sprague 2021, second-hand. Silbernagel 2007, Achilles, N=38). It was never tested after surgery.
- The guardrail rule: a pain stop value is an app rule or a clinician value. It is never an evidence default. The app rule is in FR12 of `MPFL_APP_PLAN.md`.
- The words "validated" and "clinically validated" are banned for this rule.

## Decision
