**Disagreements:** Practice favors weight-bearing as tolerated (S10, R3). N1 says no consensus. S1 says both "not limited" and "gradual progression to full weight-bearing". Show all of them. Do not average. The more protective choice is partial weight-bearing until the surgeon sets the status. This is not an evidence default. Weight-bearing as tolerated is practice. It is not a consensus. Do not present it as the default.

**Combined surgery:** If the patient had a TTO, trochleoplasty or cartilage procedure, do not use any isolated MPFLR value. A surgeon protocol is required.

**What to put in the table:** One field, "Weight-bearing status", with the value "Set by the surgeon" (and crutch use, same). Do not add a percent column. Do not let the app change the status. A change of status is a surgeon decision.

**Ask your surgeon**
- Weight-bearing status and crutch use (`research/clinical.md` section 9, item 2).
- Brace use, locked or open, and the end date (item 3).
- Any change for a concurrent procedure (item 4).

**Gaps:** A percent of body weight by week is NONE. No guideline, review or trial read gives one. The literature gives only the status words WBAT, PWB and NWB and the share of protocols that use them (S10, R3). BASK 2021 (G1) was not read, so it is UNVERIFIED and cannot support a rule.
