PICO: isolated soft-tissue MPFL surgery (repair, reconstruction or revision), ages 8 to 25 (the P2 population), all weeks after surgery. Question: is P2 a strong consensus guideline that the app can use as fixed limits.

Answer: No. P2 is not strong. The quotes of P2 are accurate, but the independent audit grades P2 items as OPINION in substance, with weak strength. The app can show P2 values as a reference for the clinician. It must never use them as fixed limits.

Why P2 cannot give fixed limits:
- The audit grades P2 rehab items "OPINION of one institution team, weak strength, 2015". Its app use is "Reference value for the clinician. Never a fixed limit".
- P2 excludes realignment surgery: "tibial tubercle re-alignment" and "lateral retinacular release". Never apply P2 to combined surgery. A surgeon protocol is required.
- P2 covers ages 8 to 25. For adults, only P1, P3, S1 and N3 apply, and all are OPINION.
- Other sources widen the range. N3 (2024, read in the audit) gives passive flexion above 125 degrees in weeks 0 to 6 and mini squats of 0 to 50 degrees. P3 allows open-chain quadriceps work from 2 weeks. P1 gives no explicit 90 degree cap.
- The audit says the knowledge base is fit for clinical rules only if every number is a clinician-set protocol field. No rule rests on outcome evidence after MPFLR.
- The 2D camera error (about 9 to 10 degrees as a working margin) is larger than some P2 values, for example the SLR lag of 10 degrees or less (Table 2, level 5). A camera cannot enforce it.

What the app can encode (audit section 4, conditions in section 6):
- Protective defaults and referrals only: no open-chain knee extension from 30 to 0 degrees in phase 1 unless a clinician enables it (P2 rec 29, with P1), the combined-surgery block, and the red-flag referral (P2 rec 4).
- Doses, angles, weeks and pain values (2 sets of 10 to 15 repetitions, flexion goals, SLR lag, pain 2 of 10): SAFE ONLY AS A CLINICIAN-SET VALUE. Show them as an example that the clinician confirms.

Ask your surgeon:
- Which limits apply to my knee and my surgery?

Gaps:
- Outcome evidence for any P2 limit after MPFLR: NONE. The literature says no validated protocol exists (S1) and only one randomised trial on rehabilitation exists among 85 studies (S2). Practice reviews (R1 to R3, S10) show the range of practice. They cannot set a rule.
