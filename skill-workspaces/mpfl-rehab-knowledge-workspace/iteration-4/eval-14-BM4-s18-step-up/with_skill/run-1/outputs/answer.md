PICO: Surgery type not stated (this answer covers isolated MPFL reconstruction). Age group not stated. Weeks since surgery not stated. Question: what S18 gives for the forward step-up.

## Answer

S18 gives a peak patellofemoral joint stress of 11.2 MPa (SD 0.6) for the forward step-up (FSU).
The same abstract gives 11.5 MPa (SD 0.8) for the lateral step-up (LSU) and 13.8 MPa (SD 0.4) for the forward step-down (FSD).
The forward step-up loads the joint less than the forward step-down.
These values come from healthy adults. They are BORROWED. They do not tell you the load on an MPFL graft.

- Correction C6 was first marked as an error and then withdrawn on 2026-11-08. The value 11.2 MPa is EXACT in the abstract.
- S18 is from 2011. It is older than 5 years.
- The P value of .002 compares the FSD with the FSU. The abstract quote gives no test between the FSU and the LSU.
- `research/clinical.md` section 4 says the stress was measured "at 45 degrees of knee flexion". That detail is not in the abstract quote above. Treat the knee angle as UNVERIFIED.
- Only the abstract was read. The abstract alone cannot support an app rule.

## Disagreements

No source in the knowledge base disagrees with S18 on the step-up.

## Ask your surgeon

- Whether step-ups are allowed for you, from which week, and at what step height.
- The maximum load and depth for closed-chain work.
These are surgeon decisions (`research/clinical.md` section 9). The app sets no default.

## Gaps

- Graft strain during a step-up after MPFL reconstruction: NONE. No study measured it. The literature gives only stress in healthy joints (S14 to S18, N23).
- A safe step height or number of reps for step-ups after MPFL surgery: NONE. S18 reports stress values. It sets no limit.
- Step-up and step-down both appear in `research/clinical.md` section 4 under phase 3 as exercise entries. The phase for each is not set by S18. Phase choice is a surgeon decision.
