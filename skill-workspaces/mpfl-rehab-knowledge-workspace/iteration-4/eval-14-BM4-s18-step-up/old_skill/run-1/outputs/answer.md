# S18 and the forward step-up

**Short answer:** S18 gives a peak patellofemoral joint stress (PFJS) of 11.2 ± 0.6 MPa for the forward step-up (FSU).

**Source:** S18 is Chinkulprasert C et al., J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 2011;41(4):241-248, PMID 21289449. It is a lab study of 20 healthy adults. See `research/clinical.md` section 4 (S18 row in the source list and the "Evidence on loading by knee angle" list).

**Values in the S18 abstract** (`research/source-audit.md`, correction C6 and the S18 row):

| Exercise | Peak PFJS (mean ± SD) |
|---|---|
| Forward step-down (FSD) | 13.8 ± 0.4 MPa |
| Lateral step-up (LSU) | 11.5 ± 0.8 MPa |
| Forward step-up (FSU) | 11.2 ± 0.6 MPa |

- The abstract says PFJS was significantly greater during the FSD than during the LSU (P<.001) and the FSU (P = .002).
- The abstract text does not report a difference between the FSU and the LSU in the quoted lines.
- The 11.2 MPa value is exact in the abstract. The first audit said it was not. The audit withdrew that claim on 2026-11-08 (C6).

**Evidence marks:**
- Access: V-abstract. The full text is behind a paywall and was not opened (`source-audit.md`, `search-log.md`).
- Population: BORROWED. The subjects were healthy adults, not MPFLR patients. The link to MPFL graft safety or outcome is UNVERIFIED (`clinical.md` section 4).
- Design: lab study, N = 20.
- Source date: 2011. This is older than 5 years. A newer study needs a search.

**Limits:**
- `clinical.md` says the comparison is "at 45 degrees of knee flexion". The audit lists the S18 angle details as paywalled, so this angle is not confirmed from the abstract. Treat it as UNVERIFIED.
- The knowledge base has no graft-strain data for exercise after MPFLR. These numbers describe stress in healthy knees only.
- This is not advice on whether, when or how to do a step-up after surgery. For that, ask your surgeon or care team.
