PICO: Revision MPFL reconstruction. Age group not stated. Weeks since surgery not stated. It is not stated if the revision was isolated or combined with a bone or cartilage procedure. Question: what rehab rules apply after revision MPFLR?

## Answer

The knowledge base has no rehab rule for revision MPFLR. The answer is NONE. A surgeon protocol is required. The app must not apply the isolated MPFLR values to a revision case.

## Reading

- A revision case can include a bone or cartilage procedure. If it does, the combined surgery block applies. The app must require a surgeon protocol.
- The P2 phase goals were written for isolated soft-tissue surgery. P2 names revision in its target group but gives no rule for it. Its goals are level 5 and from 2015. They are a reference value for the clinician. They are not a limit for a revision patient.
- The general values (flexion, weight-bearing, brace, dose) come from isolated MPFLR protocols. For a revision patient, the population is not the same. They are not supported for this group.
- Weight-bearing and brace have no consensus even for isolated MPFLR (N1: "no consensus on postoperative immobilization or weightbearing"). The app sets no default.

## Disagreements

None can be shown. No source gives a value for revision, so no values exist to compare.

## Ask your surgeon

- The full protocol for this revision: weeks, phases, and the permission to start each phase.
- Weight-bearing status and crutch use.
- Brace use, and the end date.
- The daily flexion limit and the start date of motion.
- The load and squat depth limits.
- Whether a bone or cartilage procedure was done at the same time.
- When to run, jump or return to sport.

## Gaps

- Rehab rules for revision MPFLR: NONE. The literature on primary MPFLR gives guideline goals at level 5 (P2, P1, P3, S1). It gives no revision rule.
- A validated rehab protocol after MPFLR: NONE (S1, S2).
- G1 (BASK 2021) was not read. It is UNVERIFIED and cannot support a rule.
