# ISAKOS 2013 return-to-play list: complete? primary document?

Short answer: no to both.

## Not complete

- The list in the knowledge base has 6 items. It is in `research/rehab-rules.md` section 6 (row "ISAKOS 2013 criteria", source IS1).
- The citation audit (`research/citation-audit.md`, V69) found that the source table has 10 rows. The 6 quotes are exact, but the list is PARAPHRASED (partial).
- Omitted rows: neuromuscular training, core strength, dynamic control (Star Excursion), and physiotherapist sport drills.
- The audit summary (line 153) rates it: "CONSENSUS, committee consensus, second-hand, list incomplete".
- The 6 items that the knowledge base holds: complete radiographic healing of bone if bony surgery is involved, no knee pain or instability complaints, full or near full range of motion, no knee effusion, limb symmetry index above 85 percent on hop tests, and psychological readiness (for example SANE score above 80 out of 100).

## Not read from the primary document

- The primary document (ISAKOS Sports Medicine Committee, Consensus Meeting on Return-to-Play, London 2013) was not found. `rehab-rules.md` (row IS1) says: "Second-hand: read only as Table 1 of ME1. The primary document was not found". `citation-audit.md` V94 says SOURCE NOT FOUND. `search-log.md` says the same.
- The list comes from ME1: Menetrey J, Putman S, Gard S. Knee Surg Sports Traumatol Arthrosc 2014;22(10):2320-2326, PMC4169614. It is a review, LoE IV. It prints the ISAKOS list as Table 1, with permission of the authors. The file is open access, and the knowledge base opened the full text (access mark V for ME1). The ISAKOS list itself is V-second-hand (read in another review).
- `research/source-audit.md` (C16) corrects an older note in `clinical-update.md` ("seen only in a search result, UNVERIFIED"). It now counts as VERIFIED second-hand as printed in ME1. Line 298 of `clinical-update.md` still shows the old UNVERIFIED mark. The two files differ on the mark, and the newer audit note is the correction.

## Other points

- Population: the list is for patellofemoral instability in general (dislocation or surgery). It is not specific to MPFL reconstruction (class: BORROWED or wider than MPFLR). The date is 2013, more than 5 years old. A newer version needs a search.
- The knowledge base found no ISAKOS guideline on MPFLR rehabilitation (`research/clinical.md`, line 179; `clinical-update.md`, line 183).
- The hop threshold "greater than 85 percent" is second-hand. Other sources give different values: P2 at least 85, S1 at least 90 (`research/specialists-brief.md`, RS2). The knowledge base does not average them.
- Use it as a clinician checklist only. The app cannot measure most items. Release of return to sport is a surgeon decision (ask your surgeon).
- To close the gap: get the primary ISAKOS document, or the full 10-row Table 1 of ME1, and record the opened URL and access mark.
