Why the list is not complete:
- The knowledge base shows 6 of 10 rows. The audit asks for a correction: the list is PARAPHRASED, not complete (condition 4 of `citation-audit.md` section 6 names V69).
- The 4 omitted rows are about training and function. They are not in the knowledge base, so the knowledge base cannot say how ISAKOS words them. The wording of those 4 rows is not in the knowledge base. A source that can answer is Ménétrey 2014 Table 1, or the ISAKOS committee document.

Why it is second-hand:
- The ISAKOS Sports Medicine Committee consensus (London, 2013) was not found by the audit or by the earlier agent. The earlier note said "seen only in a search result". Correction C16 moved it to "VERIFIED second-hand" because ME1 prints it with permission of the authors.
- Second-hand means the app cannot be sure that ME1 copied every row and every word exactly. The audit checked only that ME1 prints these quotes.

How to use it:
- The source is from 2013. It is older than 5 years. Flag it.
- ME1 itself (2014, review, level of evidence IV) says "no clear criteria can be as yet endorsed". No validated return-to-sport battery exists after MPFLR (S1, N4, S6).
- The audit grades it as committee consensus, second-hand, list incomplete. Use: a clinician checklist. The app cannot measure it.
- Thresholds disagree across sources: 85 percent (P2 and ISAKOS hop LSI), 90 percent (S1, ME1), 95 percent (P1, P4 as quoted in the base). The conservative value is 90 percent or more. I do not average them.
- The app cannot measure strength LSI, hop tests, effusion grade or psychological scales. A webcam comparison is "movement symmetry", never "LSI".
- Mixed population: ISAKOS covers dislocation and surgery together, and bone surgery is included.

Ask your surgeon:
- Which return-to-sport tests apply to me, and which limits?
