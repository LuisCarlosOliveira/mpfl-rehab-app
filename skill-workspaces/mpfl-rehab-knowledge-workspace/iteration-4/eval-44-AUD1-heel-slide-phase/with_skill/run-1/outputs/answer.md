PICO: isolated MPFL reconstruction (ISO), adult or adolescent, weeks 0 to 6 after surgery. Question: which sources put the heel slide in phase 1, and can the app list it there.

Answer: Two sources put the heel slide in phase 1: S1 and P3. P1 does not. The app can list the heel slide in phase 1 only as an option that the surgeon or clinician enables. It cannot present it as a fixed rule. Both phase 1 sources are OPINION in substance.

Disagreements:
- The older line in `rehab-rules.md` section 2 says "P1: heel slides in the AROM progression". The independent audit marked this WRONG (V37). That line is blocked. Do not cite P1 for phase 1.
- S1 and P3 say phase 1. P1 says phase 2, once knee flexion is above 120 degrees. The conservative timing is the later start (P1). I do not average the sources.
- The week bands also differ. S1 phase 1 is 0 to 6 weeks. P3 phase 1 is "Immediate post-op to 2 weeks". So "phase 1" does not mean the same weeks in each source.
- Other sources about range: P1 has no explicit 90 degree cap in weeks 0 to 4, but says "Avoid KF ROM beyond surgeon guidelines". P2 gives 90 degrees of passive flexion by 4 weeks as a goal. N3 (2024, read in the audit) allows passive flexion above 125 degrees in weeks 0 to 6. The range ceiling is a surgeon value.

What the app can do:
- It can list the heel slide as an exercise that a clinician sets for phase 1. The template shows the sources S1 and P3 as reference, and shows that P1 starts it later.
- The range ceiling is a clinician field. The knowledge base gives a range with sources, not one number. S1 gives "Typically, patients start 0°-30° in the first or second week and progress by 20°- 30° per week" (OPINION, VERIFIED).
- The app cannot compare a camera angle with the P2 flexion targets. Those targets are passive range (PROM). A camera sees active motion.
- A 2D knee angle error of about 9 to 10 degrees is a working margin. A smaller difference is not measurable.
- The app never changes the phase. Phase change is a surgeon decision.
- For combined surgery (TTO, trochleoplasty, cartilage), do not use this template. A surgeon protocol is required.

Ask your surgeon:
- Are heel slides allowed now, and to which knee angle?
- When can the range go past the first limit?

Gaps:
- Evidence of benefit or harm for heel slides in phase 1 after MPFLR: NONE. No trial compares heel slide timing. The literature says no validated protocol exists (S1) and only one randomised trial on rehabilitation exists among 85 studies (S2).
- BASK 2021 (G1) was not read. It is UNVERIFIED and cannot support a rule.
