# Can the app cite the N1 Delphi "full ROM by 6 weeks" as a rule?

Short answer: no. The app cannot cite it as a rule, and it cannot quote it as N1 wording.

## Why not

1. The wording is second-hand. N1 is Hurley ET et al., "A modified Delphi consensus statement on patellar instability: part II", Bone Joint J 2023;105-B(12):1265-1270 (60 surgeons, 11 countries, modified Delphi). The knowledge base read only the abstract (V-abstract). The publisher page is behind a paywall, so the statement text was not opened (`research/search-log.md` A10, `research/citation-audit.md` V91 NOT READABLE, `research/clinical-update.md` section on N1).
2. The phrase comes from S10 (Vidra 2026, PMC13247021), which reports N1. The citation audit (V9) matches the S10 quote "full ROM should be achieved by 6 weeks after MPFL repair or reconstruction" as EXACT to S10. It is V-second-hand for N1. `clinical-update.md` notes that S10 paraphrases the N1 wording. So "N1 said this" is UNVERIFIED as wording. Only "S10 reports that N1 said this" is supported.
3. A Delphi consensus is not a rule. The audit rates N1 via S10 as "CONSENSUS of 60 surgeons, wording unread. Supports early motion as a principle only" (`citation-audit.md`, line 152). The population is surgeons' opinion (OPINION class), not a trial.
4. The sources disagree on the timing. `research/rehab-rules.md` ("Full flexion timing" row) shows: N1 via S10, by 6 weeks. S1 conclusion: full knee ROM and weight-bearing "at six weeks". S1 text: full knee flexion "between 4 to 12 weeks". S10 Table 2 (35 isolated protocols): full ROM by 0 to 6 weeks in 12 (37 percent), by 6 to 12 weeks in 18 (51 percent), after 12 weeks in 2 (6 percent). R2: full ROM by week 6 in 15 of the isolated protocols (48.4 percent). The file marks "Agree?" as No (6 weeks to 12 weeks) and the rule as "No single value. Show the protocol value only". The mark is CONSENSUS (Delphi, second-hand, UNVERIFIED wording) and REVIEW (practice counts only).
5. The same Delphi found no consensus on immobilization or weight-bearing (abstract, V-abstract). So N1 does not support a full protocol rule.
6. It is a clinician decision. The start and the daily limit of knee flexion in weeks 0 to 6 is on the "ask your surgeon" list (`research/clinical.md` section 9, item 1). The app must not set a default.

## What the app can say

- It can say, with the marks: "A modified Delphi (N1, 60 surgeons), as reported by a 2026 review (S10), supports early motion, and says full ROM should be achieved by 6 weeks after MPFL repair or reconstruction. The wording of N1 was not read. Practice ranges from 6 to over 12 weeks. Ask your surgeon." The audit says this supports early motion as a principle only.
- Case: surgery type is MPFL repair or reconstruction as the review states it. The population split (isolated or combined, adult or adolescent) is not given for the statement.
- Date: 2023 (N1), 2026 (S10). Both are under 5 years old.
- It cannot be used as a pass or fail threshold, a target, or a default. A "full ROM by 6 weeks" number in the app would be a surgeon value or a PROPOSAL that needs clinician review.

## To close the gap

Open the N1 statements (full text or supplement) and record the opened URL and access mark. Until then the wording stays V-second-hand.
