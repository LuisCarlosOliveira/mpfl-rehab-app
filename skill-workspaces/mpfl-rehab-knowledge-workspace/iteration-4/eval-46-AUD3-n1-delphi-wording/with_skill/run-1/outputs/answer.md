PICO: isolated MPFL reconstruction (ISO), adult and adolescent, weeks 0 to 12 after surgery. Question: can the app cite the N1 Delphi statement "full ROM by 6 weeks" as a rule.

Answer: No. The app cannot cite it as a rule. The wording of N1 is second-hand and the primary text of N1 was not readable. The app can cite it only as support for the principle of early motion, and it must say that it is second-hand.

Why the app cannot use it as a rule:
- An unread source is UNVERIFIED and cannot support an app rule. The wording that the knowledge base holds comes through S10, not from N1.
- The independent audit marks V91 NOT READABLE. A row of that kind is not usable for a number, a quote or an app rule. The audit blocked the primary text.
- The audit grades N1 via S10 as "CONSENSUS of 60 surgeons, wording unread". The use that it allows is "Supports early motion as a principle only".
- The sources disagree on timing. Six weeks (N1 via S10, S1 conclusion) versus 6 to 12 weeks and 4 to 12 weeks (S1 text, S10 Table 2, R2). `rehab-rules.md` section 1 gives no single value. I do not average them.
- Two sides of risk: too little motion leads to stiffness and arthrofibrosis (S1, N22). Too much motion strains the graft. The date and the range stay a surgeon decision.
- The app never changes the phase. A "full ROM by 6 weeks" message to a patient would act as a goal or a deadline. This is not allowed.
- P2 flexion targets (90 degrees by 4 weeks, 120 degrees by 6 weeks) are passive range (PROM). A camera sees active motion. Do not compare them as one quantity.
- Combined surgery (TTO and others): do not apply this. A surgeon protocol is required.

What the app can do:
- Show "early motion is supported" with the note "Delphi consensus, second-hand through S10, wording not verified". Add the practice counts as a range.
- Make the ROM target date a clinician field. Show the sources as reference.

Ask your surgeon:
- When must my knee reach full motion, and what do I do if it does not?

Gaps:
- Primary wording of the N1 rehab statements: not in the knowledge base. A source that can answer is the full text of N1 (Bone and Joint Journal). Ask for the link.
- BASK 2021 (G1) and AWMF S2e 012-024 are also not readable (V92, V93). They cannot fill this gap.
