# Valgus angle for "bad form"

Not in the knowledge base as a valid threshold. The app must not show a "bad form" verdict.

- form-opus.md section 1: no study gives a validated valgus threshold for patients after MPFLR or with patellar instability. Existing cut-offs come from patellofemoral pain or ACL screening, one small study each. They are BORROWED. In ACL work, 3D valgus did not predict injury in 710 athletes (Krosshaug 2016).
- Criteria table: C9 (absolute valgus or FPPA against 8.2 or 5.2 degrees) is BORROWED, not validated, bias up to 19 degrees. "Never show". C15 (any "good form", "bad form", score or colour verdict): evidence NONE, "Never show".
- Measurement: Asaeda 2024 (V, 15 healthy men, Vicon, MediaPipe 2D) found an absolute valgus bias of -19.28 degrees. The change from the start of the rep agreed better (bias 0.181), but test-retest ICC was 0.14 to 0.70.
- What is allowed: C8, valgus change from the start of the rep, front view, as a record for the clinician. No live cue. BORROWED (PFP), NONE for MPFLR. It waits for a clinician limit and the T13 margin test.
- Context: Dewan 2020 (13 videos) saw valgus with a flexed knee in all 10 non-contact dislocations. It does not prove that valgus in rehab causes recurrence.

If a number is needed, the clinician sets it. Any value I give is a PROPOSAL and needs clinician review.
