# Week 10, knee feels fine: can the app say "start running"?

No. Permission to run is a surgeon decision (research/clinical.md section 9, item 8). A patient who says "it feels fine" is not a clearance.

- Sources do not agree. Dartmouth Hitchcock allows running at 12 weeks (P6). HSS needs surgeon clearance and 1.7 Nm/kg (P1). NHS Lothian needs 90 percent strength and hops first (P3).
- S1 gives run-start criteria: full ROM or more than 95 percent of the other knee, pain below 2 of 10, quadriceps LSI more than 70 percent (isometric). This is a single-source claim (clinical.md section 3). The webcam cannot measure LSI.
- The app must block progress to a new phase until the user reports surgeon clearance (clinical.md section 7).

Suggested wording: "Running needs approval from your surgeon or physiotherapist. Ask at your next visit." The app can log the pain and swelling trend to show them.
