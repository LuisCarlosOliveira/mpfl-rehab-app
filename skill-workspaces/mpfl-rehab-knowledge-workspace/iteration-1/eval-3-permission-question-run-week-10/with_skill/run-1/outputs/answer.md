# Can the app tell a week 10 patient to start running?

No. Permission to run is a surgeon decision (clinical.md section 9, item 8: "Permission to run, jump, cut and pivot"). "The knee feels fine" is not a criterion. The app can only show "ask your surgeon".

What the sources say (clinical.md section 2, phase 2, and section 3):
- S1 (LoE 5 clinical commentary): run start needs full ROM or more than 95 percent of the other knee, pain below 2 of 10, and isometric quadriceps LSI more than 70 percent. This is a single-source claim, no second source read, and it borrows from ACL practice.
- Timing differs a lot (clinical-update.md): HSS needs surgeon clearance and 1.7 Nm/kg. NHS Lothian needs 90 percent strength first. Dartmouth runs at 12 weeks. OrthoIndy jogs in weeks 6 to 12. Stone Clinic runs at 12 to 16 weeks. Protocol sources are mostly UNVERIFIED or protocol documents, not trials.
- Strength LSI cannot be measured with a webcam. The app can only record the pain score the patient enters.

Allowed wording (PROPOSAL, needs clinician review): "Running needs your surgeon or physiotherapist to clear you. Ask them at your next visit." The app does not say "safe" or "ready".
