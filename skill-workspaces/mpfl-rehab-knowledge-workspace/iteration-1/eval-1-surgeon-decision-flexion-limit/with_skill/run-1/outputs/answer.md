# Maximum knee flexion in weeks 0 to 6

Ask your surgeon. The app must not set a default maximum flexion. The knowledge base does not give one number.

- clinical.md section 9, item 1: "The start date and the daily limit of knee flexion in weeks 0 to 6" is a surgeon decision. The app shows "ask your surgeon".
- Sources disagree on ROM limits (clinical.md section 3, ROM limits). S2 (systematic review, 85 studies): more than half of programmes limited ROM to 90 degrees or less within 3 weeks. S3 (systematic review, 53 studies): ROM restriction in 84.9 percent of studies. S1 (LoE 5 review): start at 0 to 30 degrees in weeks 1 to 2, then +20 to 30 degrees a week. No trial compares limits.
- Institution protocols (clinical.md section 6, UNVERIFIED, or clinical-update.md): HSS says 90 by week 4 and 120 by week 6. Dartmouth says no limit. Stone Clinic says no flexion past 90 for 4 weeks. These are examples, not a rule.

Design suggestion (PROPOSAL, needs clinician review): the clinician or surgeon enters the ceiling for the patient. The screen shows it and the margin rule applies (form-opus.md section 4.3, criterion C1, start margin 9 degrees, UNVERIFIED for the lite model). The webcam angle is an estimate, not a medical measurement.
