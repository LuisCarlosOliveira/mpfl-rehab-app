**PICO:** Isolated MPFLR (the question does not say). Age group not stated. Weeks since surgery not stated (return to sport is usually at 6 to 8 months or later). Question: is there a validated return-to-sport (RTS) battery?

**Answer:** No. The knowledge base finds no validated RTS battery after MPFLR. Only consensus lists and borrowed ACL thresholds exist. Do not write "validated" for any of them.

Notes on the ISAKOS list:
- Table 1 in ME1 has 10 rows. The knowledge base quotes only 6 (citation-audit V69, PARAPHRASED). The omitted rows are neuromuscular training, core strength, dynamic control (Star Excursion) and physiotherapist sport drills.
- The primary ISAKOS document was not found (citation-audit V94, SOURCE NOT FOUND).
- A consensus list is not a validated battery. No MPFLR study links passing these tests to fewer re-injuries (S6).

**Disagreements (thresholds in use):** Show each value with its source. Never average.
- 85 percent: P2 (hop LSI "≥ 85", 2015, level 5, so OPINION in substance, older than 5 years) and IS1 (hop LSI "> 85 %", second-hand).
- 90 percent: S1 (hop LSI "≥ 90%", Y-Balance "≥ 90% on each limb and side-to-side difference of < 4cm", isometric peak torque "≥ 90%"). ME1 ("LSI of at least 90 %" for high-demand sports). P3 (NHS Lothian 2022): "Two functional hop tests ... > 90% of unaffected limb" (citation-audit V66).
- 95 percent: P1 and P4 (the base lists the figure, protocols, OPINION).
- Conservative value: 90 percent or more for strength and hops (S1, ME1).
- The S1 rules for Y-Balance and hop tests are BORROWED from ACL work. Run start differs too: S1 gives 70 percent quadriceps LSI (BORROWED from an ACL review, C3). P3 gives 90 percent strength. The conservative value is 90 percent.

**Ask your surgeon:**
- Which tests and which cutoffs does my surgeon use to clear return to sport?
- Does my surgery include a bone procedure (TTO or trochleoplasty)? If yes, a surgeon protocol is required. Isolated MPFLR rules do not apply.
- Final RTS clearance and its criteria are a surgeon decision.

**Gaps:**
- NONE: a validated RTS battery after MPFLR. The literature gives consensus lists (ISAKOS 2013 via ME1), a Delphi checklist (N4, domains not read), and thresholds borrowed from ACL work (S1, AS1).
- NONE: a cutoff proven for MPFLR for strength LSI, hop LSI, Y-Balance or MPFL-RSI.
- NONE: an MPFLR study that links passing a battery to lower re-injury (S6).
- Time to RTS is only descriptive: about 6 to 8 months (S4 7.0 months, S5 6.7 months, S1 7 to 8 months, REVIEW). It is not a target.
- The ISAKOS source is second-hand and UNVERIFIED as a primary document, so it cannot support an app rule alone. N4 full domains are UNVERIFIED (paywall).

**For the app:** The app cannot measure strength LSI, hop tests, Y-Balance, gait or effusion grade. These stay clinician inputs. A webcam left-right comparison is "movement symmetry", never "LSI". Any RTS cutoff in the app is a protocol field that a clinician sets (citation-audit section 6). The knowledge base values serve only as reference ranges.
