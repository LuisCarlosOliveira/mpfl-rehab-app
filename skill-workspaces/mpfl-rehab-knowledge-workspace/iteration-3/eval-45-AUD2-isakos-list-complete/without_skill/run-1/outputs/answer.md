**Not complete.** The list in `rehab-rules.md` section 6 has 6 items:
- complete radiographic healing of bone, if bony surgery is involved
- no knee pain or knee instability
- full or near full range of motion
- no knee effusion
- Limb Symmetry Index > 85 % on hop tests, especially if resuming pivoting sports
- psychological readiness (for example SANE score > 80/100)

Table 1 in ME1 has 10 rows. The citation audit (V69) rates the KB list PARAPHRASED. Each quote is exact, but four rows are left out:
