## PICO

Surgery: revision MPFL reconstruction (MPFLR). Age group and weeks since surgery: not stated, so the answer covers no specific case. Question: which rehab rules apply after revision MPFLR.

## Answer

The knowledge base has no rehab rule for revision MPFLR. The status is NONE.

I cannot give you a revision phase plan, a weight-bearing rule, a brace rule or a range of motion target. I will not copy the isolated primary MPFLR rules and call them revision rules. A surgeon protocol is required.

Because P2 names revision but gives no separate rule, any P2 number is an isolated-MPFLR value. P2 grades almost all its own items as level 5, which is expert opinion. The audit states that no P2 revision exists after 2015 (`research/citation-audit.md`, section 3). Even for isolated primary surgery, every P2 value is a reference value for the clinician and never a fixed limit.

Revision cases can also include other procedures. P2 excludes realignment surgery (such as TTO). If your revision includes a TTO, trochleoplasty or cartilage procedure, the isolated rules do not apply (`research/rehab-rules.md`, section 8).

## Disagreements

None can be shown. No source gives a revision-specific value, so there is nothing to compare. The conservative position is to follow the written protocol of your surgeon.

## Ask your surgeon

- Which phases and weeks apply to my revision.
- Weight-bearing status and brace use, and for how long.
- The knee flexion limit and the date it can change.
- Which exercises are allowed now, and which are not.
- Whether my revision included any other procedure (realignment, trochleoplasty, cartilage).
- Which signs mean I must call the care team.

## Gaps

- Revision MPFLR rehab: NONE. The knowledge base found no source for it. The BASK 2021 guideline (G1) and the German AWMF guideline were not readable (paywall or no usable file). They are UNVERIFIED and cannot support a rule. They may contain relevant text, but I did not read them.
- Isolated primary MPFLR rules exist in `research/rehab-rules.md` (sections 1 to 5). They are mostly OPINION (level 5). They are not validated for revision, and I do not apply them here.
- Phase changes are a surgeon decision. The app never changes the phase.
- To close the gap, a clinician needs to write a revision protocol, or a source specific to revision MPFLR needs to be found, opened and read.

If you have a symptom now (new swelling, calf pain, fever, a knee that locks), contact your care team now. For the emergency list, call emergency services.
