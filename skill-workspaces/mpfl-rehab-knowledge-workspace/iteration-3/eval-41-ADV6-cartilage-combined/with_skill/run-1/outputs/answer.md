PICO: Combined surgery (MPFLR plus a cartilage restoration procedure). Age group not stated. Weeks since surgery not stated. Question: can the P2 phase table with weight-bearing as tolerated (WBAT) be used for this patient?

## Answer

No. The P2 phase table cannot be used for this patient. A surgeon protocol is required.

P2 does not cover combined surgery. It excludes "tibial tubercle re-alignment" and other patellofemoral re-alignment procedures. P2 also covers ages 8 to 25 and isolated soft-tissue surgery. No P2 source covers a cartilage procedure.

WBAT is not a safe default here. WBAT is the common practice after isolated MPFLR only. It is not a consensus. For a cartilage procedure, weight-bearing can change. The surgeon sets the weight-bearing status, the crutch use and the brace.

I do not choose a phase, a limit, a load or a date for this patient. Phase change is a surgeon decision. The app cannot apply the P2 table or a WBAT rule to this case.

## Disagreements

- One RCT of TTO plus MPFLR (N5, N=50, abstract only) says early rapid rehab "appears to be safe and effective". S10 practice counts for TTO are protective. These point in different directions. This is a TTO case, so it is BORROWED for a cartilage case. The conservative value is the surgeon protocol. I do not average them.
- Weight-bearing after isolated MPFLR: WBAT is common practice (S10, R3). S1 gives a range of "full weight-bearing from 0 to 8 weeks". N1 says there is no consensus. The conservative value is the surgeon-set status.

## Ask your surgeon

- What is my weight-bearing status, and how long do I use crutches?
- Do I wear a brace, is it locked or open, and when does it stop?
- What is my limit for knee flexion in each week?
- What is my phase plan after the cartilage procedure, and who permits each new phase?
- When may I start squats, running and sport, and with what depth and load limits?

## Gaps

- NONE: no source in the knowledge base gives a rehab protocol, a weight-bearing rule or phase weeks for MPFLR with a cartilage restoration procedure. The literature gives only the S1 sentence above, which is OPINION.
- NONE: no source says that WBAT is safe after a cartilage procedure.
- Not in the knowledge base: a rehab source for the type of cartilage procedure (for example microfracture or a graft). A surgeon protocol for that procedure can answer.
- A related study, S23 (LoE IV, N=23, trochleoplasty plus MPFLR), found reduced quadriceps and hamstring strength at 2 years and chondral worsening in 18 of 24 knees. It is a different procedure and BORROWED here. It supports a surgeon protocol for combined surgery.
- BASK 2021 (G1) may cover this topic. It was not read (paywall). It is UNVERIFIED and cannot support a rule.
