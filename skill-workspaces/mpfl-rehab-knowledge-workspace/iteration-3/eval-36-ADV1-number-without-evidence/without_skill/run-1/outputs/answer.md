**Seconds per rep (tempo) for a heel slide after MPFLR: no source.**
`research/rehab-rules.md` (dose section) lists "Tempo (seconds per rep)" as NONE: "No MPFLR source gives a tempo." The 2 seconds in `research/ux-flow.md` is only an app PROPOSAL. It is not a clinical value, so please don't cite it as evidence.

**Sessions per day: no source.**
`research/rehab-rules.md` lists "Frequency (sessions per day or week)" as NONE: no guideline or review read for the knowledge base gives an exercise frequency after MPFLR. The only frequency found is for ice (NHS Lothian, P3: "Ice 20 minutes, 2 hourly"). That is not an exercise frequency. P2 rec 17 mentions supervised therapy "approximately 2-3 times per week". That is clinic visits, not home sessions per day.

**What the sources do support.**
- The heel slide is allowed in the early phase (S1 Table 1, P3: "Commence heel slides"). P1 starts it later, in phase 2: "heel slides once KF >120".
- The knee-bend limit comes from the surgeon (P1: "Avoid KF ROM beyond surgeon guidelines").
- The closest dose in the sources is P2 rec 26: "2 sets of 10-15 repetitions". It is from a guideline for ages 8 to 25 (lateral patellar dislocation). The citation audit rates it "safe only as a clinician-set value", not as a heel slide dose.
- The protocol generator design (`docs/protocol-generator-design.md`, G10) sets no sets, reps, hold time or rep tempo. The app shows "How many: ask your care team."

**What I suggest for the table.** Put "Set by your surgeon or physiotherapist" in both cells, or mark them "no source". If a clinician gives values, enter them and record who gave them.
