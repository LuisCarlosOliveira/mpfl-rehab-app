No. The project evidence does not show that Sword Health proved camera feedback after knee surgery.

What the sources say:

- The Sword study on knee surgery is Correia 2018 (total knee arthroplasty, TKA). The full text was read (PMC6062628). It used "inertial motion trackers", not a camera.
- It was a feasibility study, not an RCT. Allocation "was performed according to geographical criteria, and not through randomization". The earlier project notes called it an RCT-like result. Correction C15 in `research/source-audit.md` fixed this.
- The Sword randomised trial that the project opened is Cui 2023. It covers chronic low back pain, not the knee. It showed no difference in disability (difference -0.55, 95% CI -2.42 to 5.81). Dropout was 15.7 percent digital against 34.3 percent in person. Sword sponsored it and six authors are employees. Nobody was blinded and no objective measures were used.
- In that trial a human therapist made the adjustments to the protocol. The system did not do it by itself.
- Sword's own pages and study page were not read (HTTP 429). A search summary says the system uses sensors and vision. This is UNVERIFIED. No accuracy figure or outcome result for a camera was found.
- For the whole topic, `research/form-evidence.md` concludes: "no trial shows that live camera cues improve outcome or safety after knee surgery." A smart-device tele-rehab review after TKA (Zhai 2025, 22 RCTs) showed no clear benefit on pain (SMD 0.02) or WOMAC (SMD -0.27, CI crossing zero). It is borrowed evidence and not about cameras.

What this means for the app: Sword is useful as an idea source (sensors plus a human therapist). It is not proof for camera feedback. A camera cue is an app behavior that needs clinician approval. The app must not claim it is evidence-based. It must not claim it is proven for MPFL reconstruction either, because none of this evidence covers MPFL.

Limits: the project read only abstracts or partial pages for some sources. It did not read Sword product pages. A newer Sword camera study could exist that the project did not find.
