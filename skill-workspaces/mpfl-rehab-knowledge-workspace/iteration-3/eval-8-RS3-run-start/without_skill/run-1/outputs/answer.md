No source gives one week number for the start of running after MPFL reconstruction. Your surgeon or physiotherapist decides when you may run. The app does not decide this and cannot give you permission to run.

What the sources say is a set of criteria, not a date:

- Range, pain and strength (S1): full range of motion, or more than 95 percent of the other knee. No pain, or pain below 2 out of 10. Quadriceps strength of more than 70 percent of the other leg. These criteria come from an ACL review, so they are borrowed from ACL rehab. They are not validated for MPFL.
- Strength (P3, NHS Lothian MPFL guideline): strength must be 90 percent of the unaffected side before jogging or running drills. P3 also asks for this before plyometrics and hop tests.
- Swelling (ACL guideline AS1, also borrowed): no effusion, or only a trace, before running.

The sources disagree on the strength threshold: 70 percent (S1) against 90 percent (P3). The cautious choice is 90 percent.

Protocols from clinics differ in timing. Examples are jogging in weeks 6 to 12 (OrthoIndy), running at 12 weeks (Dartmouth Hitchcock) and running at 12 to 16 weeks (Stone Clinic). Treat these as examples from single protocols, not as a rule. Many patients do not meet the strength thresholds at 6 to 8 months.

The app cannot measure quadriceps strength symmetry. That needs a dynamometer or a clinic test. So the app cannot tell you that you passed the running criteria.

What to do: ask your surgeon or physiotherapist for permission and for the exact tests. Running in week 10, for example, is a question for them. If you have swelling, pain or a feeling that the kneecap slips, tell them before you start.
