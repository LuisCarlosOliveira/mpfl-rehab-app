## PICO

Case: isolated MPFL reconstruction (no TTO, trochleoplasty or cartilage procedure), adult or adolescent, any week after surgery. The question asks when running can start. The question does not give your surgery type, age or week. If your surgery was combined, a surgeon protocol is required. These values do not apply.

## Answer

No source in the knowledge base gives a safe start week or date for running after MPFL reconstruction. Sources give criteria, not a date. Your surgeon or physiotherapist decides when you run. The app does not choose a date or a phase.

The criteria in the sources are about range, pain and strength. The app cannot measure strength. A clinician must test it.

## Disagreements

- Strength threshold before running: 70 percent quadriceps LSI (S1, borrowed from ACL), 80 percent (AS1, ACL), 90 percent of the other side (P3). The conservative value is 90 percent (P3). The values are not averaged.
- Time: protocols range from week 6 to 12 (P10) up to week 12 to 16 (P11), with P6 at week 12. There is no common week.
- Clearance: P1 requires surgeon clearance. S1 and P3 state criteria without naming the surgeon.

## Ask your surgeon

- Which date or test result lets me start running?
- Which strength test do you use, and what value must I reach?
- Does my graft or any other procedure change the rule?
- Do I need a brace or limits when I start?

## Gaps

- Start week for running: NONE. No source gives a validated week. No study compares time-based and criteria-based progression after MPFLR (`rehab-rules.md` section 5, "Time versus criteria": NONE for outcome evidence). The literature gives only the range of protocol practice above.
- A validated run-start or return-to-sport battery after MPFLR: NONE. S1, N4 and S6 state that criteria are not established. The run-start rule itself is BORROWED from ACL (C3).
- Many patients do not reach the criteria early: S6 "only 14% to 40% of patients met all study-specific RTS criteria" (mixed population, 41 percent had a bone procedure, correction C9). N20 reports quadriceps LSI "85.3%" at "a mean 7.4 months" (10 of 28 had a TTO). Both are REVIEW or case series, mixed populations. Time to return to sport is about 6 to 8 months (S4 "7.0 months", S5 "mean of 6.7 months"). It is descriptive, not a target.
- Psychological readiness: the MPFL-RSI cutoff of 56 (S1) is not validated for MPFLR.

## App note

The app cannot test strength, hops, effusion grade or gait. Strength and clearance stay clinician inputs. Any run-start value in the app must be a protocol field that a clinician sets, labeled borrowed from ACL. The app must not show a "ready to run" or "goal reached" message. A webcam value is "movement symmetry", not LSI.

If you have pain, swelling or other symptoms now, contact your care team now.
