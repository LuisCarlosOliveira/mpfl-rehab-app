# Flexion target at 4 weeks after MPFL reconstruction

**PICO:** The question does not say the surgery type, the age or the case. This answer covers isolated MPFL reconstruction (no bone or cartilage procedure), at 4 weeks after surgery. Question: which knee flexion target applies at 4 weeks.

## Answer

The guidelines in the knowledge base name 90 degrees or more of knee flexion by 4 weeks. This is a reference value for the clinician. It is not a fixed limit, and your surgeon sets your own target.

Two cautions apply. The evidence for this value is weak (expert opinion, level 5). The value is for passive range (PROM). A camera sees active motion, which is usually less. The app must not compare the two as one quantity.

## Disagreements

- Some protocols are more restrictive. S2 (systematic review, 2022) reports that ROM restriction to "≤ 90°" was in place within three weeks for more than half of the programmes. `rehab-rules.md` also lists a ceiling of no more than 90 degrees in weeks 0 to 4 in several protocols (P5, P11). The audit marks the P1 version of this ceiling as PARAPHRASED: P1 has no explicit 90 degree cap. P5 and P11 were not checked by the audit.
- Some sources are more permissive. N3 (Hysing-Dahl and Inderhaug 2024, read in the audit) gives for weeks 0 to 6: "Passive knee flexion of >125°". R3 gives 90 degrees at a mean of 1.4 weeks.
- Full ROM timing also differs: by 0 to 6 weeks in 37 percent of protocols, by 6 to 12 weeks in 51 percent, after 12 weeks in 6 percent (S10 Table 2, `source-audit.md` C5). S1 conflicts with itself (C2).
- Conservative value: the guidelines give 90 degrees by 4 weeks as a goal, with no single number across all sources. The more restrictive protocols limit flexion to 90 degrees or less early. No value is averaged. The surgeon sets the real number.

## Ask your surgeon

- What is my own flexion target at 4 weeks, and is it passive or active range?
- Is there a limit on flexion in the first weeks for my surgery?
- If I am not near 90 degrees by 4 weeks, when must I contact you?
- Did my surgery include a bone or cartilage procedure? If yes, a surgeon protocol replaces these values. Do not use the isolated MPFLR value for combined surgery (P2 excludes "tibial tubercle re-alignment"; P1: "Follow most conservative guideline").

## Gaps

- NONE: no source gives an outcome-based flexion target at 4 weeks. No validated rehabilitation protocol exists (S1: "no validated rehabilitation protocols are established"). The literature gives practice ranges only.
- NONE: no source gives a camera or active-angle equivalent for the passive 90 degree target. A 2D angle has an error of about 9 degrees or more in the best case (F1, `citation-audit.md` V85), so a camera cannot confirm a 90 degree target.
- UNVERIFIED: BASK 2021 consensus (G1) is paywalled and was not read. The Hurley 2023 Delphi (N1, "full ROM should be achieved by 6 weeks") is read second-hand through S10 and cannot support a rule.

This answer is information from the knowledge base only. It is not treatment advice. If you have pain, swelling or a loss of range now, contact your care team.
