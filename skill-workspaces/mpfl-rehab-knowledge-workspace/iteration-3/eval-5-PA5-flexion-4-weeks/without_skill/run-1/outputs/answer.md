The app does not set a flexion target for week 4. Your surgeon sets it. A surgeon protocol always overrides the research in this project. This is not medical advice.

What the sources say for week 4 after MPFL reconstruction:

- P2 (Cincinnati Children's Guideline 46, consensus, level 5): at least 90 degrees knee flexion PROM by 4 weeks, and at least 120 degrees by 6 weeks.
- P1 (institution protocol): about 90 degrees by 4 weeks, progressing to 120 by 6 weeks. Contact the surgeon if the range is not trending towards 90 degrees by 4 weeks.
- S1 (IJSPT 2025 review, expert opinion): start at 0 to 30 degrees in weeks 1 to 2, then add 20 to 30 degrees each week. Full flexion is reported at 4 to 12 weeks. This is a range, not one number.
- Practice across 11 protocols (P1 to P11): flexion at week 4 runs from 90 degrees (P1, P2, P4, P11) up to 130 degrees (P6).
- Some protocols (P1, P5, P11) cap flexion at 90 degrees or less in weeks 0 to 4. So 90 is a floor in some protocols and a ceiling in others. Use the number your surgeon gives you.

Limit of the numbers: the P2 targets are passive range (PROM), measured by a clinician. The app camera sees active motion. The two are not the same quantity. Do not compare a camera angle with the P2 target as if they match. The camera also has an error of about 9 degrees.

What to do: ask your surgeon or physiotherapist for your week 4 target and any limit, and enter that value in the app. If your bend is clearly below about 90 degrees at week 4, or you lose range, contact your care team.
