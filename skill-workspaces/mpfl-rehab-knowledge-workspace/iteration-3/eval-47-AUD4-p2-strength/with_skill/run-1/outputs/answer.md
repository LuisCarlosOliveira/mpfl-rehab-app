**PICO:** Isolated MPFL repair, reconstruction or revision with no realignment, ages 8 to 25 (the P2 population), weeks 0 to 12 and later. Question: can the app use P2 as strong consensus and as fixed limits?

**Answer:** No. P2 is not a strong consensus guideline. The app must not use its values as fixed limits. In substance, P2 items are OPINION. The app can show them only as reference values that a clinician confirms and sets.

**Disagreements:** Other readable sources widen the range. P3 allows open-chain quadriceps work from 2 weeks with no arc. N3 (2024) gives passive flexion above 125 degrees in weeks 0 to 6, mini squats of 0 to 50 degrees and brace-free rehab. R3 shows 90 degrees at a mean of 1.4 weeks. P1 restricts open-chain extension longer than P2 (through weeks 7 to 11 unless the surgeon clears it). The conservative value for each item is the stricter protective one. Never average the values. N1 says there is no consensus on weight-bearing or immobilization, and BASK 2021 was not read (UNVERIFIED).

**How the app must use P2** (`citation-audit.md` sections 3, 4 and 6):
- Every number (range, dose, pain value, week, lag, cutoff) is a protocol field that a clinician sets. P2 values appear only as reference ranges.
- The app enforces only protective defaults and referrals: no terminal open-chain extension in phase 1 unless a clinician enables it (P2 rec 29, P1 phase 1), the combined-surgery block, and the red-flag referral (P2 rec 4).
- The app never compares a passive P2 target with an active camera angle.
- A clinician reviews the rule set before patient use.
- No rule rests on outcome evidence after MPFLR. P1 and P3 are also OPINION. S1 is level 5.

**Ask your surgeon:**
- Which ranges, doses and week targets apply to my surgery.
- Whether my surgery was combined (bone or cartilage procedure). If so, a surgeon protocol is required.

**Gaps:**
- Validated rehab protocol after MPFLR: NONE. The literature says "no validated rehabilitation protocols are established" (S1) and "no consensus on postoperative immobilization or weightbearing" (N1).
- Outcome evidence for any P2 limit: NONE. Only indirect evidence exists for the dose (Rhea 2002 [2b], Faigenbaum 1996 [4b], other groups).
- Adult-specific P2 values: NONE. P2 covers ages 8 to 25. For adults, only P1, P3, S1 and N3 apply, and all are OPINION.
