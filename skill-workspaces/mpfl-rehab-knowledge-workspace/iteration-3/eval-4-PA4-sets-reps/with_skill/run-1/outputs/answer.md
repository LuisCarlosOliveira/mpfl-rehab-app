# Sets and reps for strengthening after MPFL reconstruction

**PICO:** Isolated MPFL reconstruction (the source covers ages 8 to 25, soft tissue only, no realignment). Weeks since surgery are not stated, so the answer covers all phases. Question: which sets and reps does the evidence give for strengthening?

## Answer

The evidence gives one dose rule. It is 2 sets of 10 to 15 repetitions, with progression to 3 sets as tolerated. This is a guideline opinion, not trial evidence after MPFL reconstruction. No adult study of dose exists. Your surgeon or physiotherapist must confirm the dose. The app can show it only as an example that a clinician sets.

## Disagreements

None found. Only P2 gives sets and reps. S1 gives intensity (RPE 6 to 8, then over 80% 1RM) and no sets or reps. The two do not conflict. The file does not give a conservative value to choose between them.

## Ask your surgeon

- Which exercises, how many sets and repetitions, and how much load for each phase.
- Maximum squat or lunge depth and load limit (`research/clinical.md` section 9, item 7).
- Permission to start each new phase (item 6).
- Whether any pain or swelling threshold pauses the program (item 14).
- If you had a bone or cartilage procedure with the MPFL (for example TTO), these values do not apply. A surgeon protocol is required.

## Gaps

- **Frequency (sessions per day or week): NONE.** No guideline or review read gives it for MPFLR. P3 gives a frequency only for ice ("Ice 20 minutes, 2 hourly"). That is not an exercise dose.
- **Tempo (seconds per rep): NONE.** No MPFLR source gives it. The 2 seconds in `ux-flow.md` is a PROPOSAL and needs clinician review.
- **Sets and reps for each phase and each exercise: NONE.** P2 gives one dose for all exercises and phases.
- **Load in kilograms or percent 1RM for phases 1 and 2: NONE.** Only RPE 6 to 8 (S1) and "over 80% 1RM" in the late phase (S1) exist.
- **Dose in adults after MPFLR: NONE.** The P2 dose rests on strength training in other groups and on local consensus.
- **Revision surgery: NONE.** No rehab source gives a separate rule.
- The 2 to 3 sets and 10 to 15 reps rule is PROPOSAL-level for any app use. The audit (`research/citation-audit.md` section 4.3) says: safe only as a clinician-set value.
