PICO: isolated MPFL reconstruction (MPFLR), age group not stated, weeks since surgery not stated. Question: does a study measure graft strain during exercise after MPFLR?

Answer: No. The knowledge base holds no study that measures graft strain during exercise after MPFLR. Graft strain number: NONE.

These stress values describe the patellofemoral joint in healthy people. They do not describe the graft. Do not use them as a graft strain value.

Disagreements: the open-chain arcs differ. P2 gives 90 to 30 degrees (a Note, "It may be helpful", V46). P1 gives 90 to 40 degrees in phase 2 (weeks 7 to 11). AS1 gives 90 to 45 degrees (ACL, BORROWED). P3 allows open-chain work from 2 weeks with no arc. N3 restricts it only after bone procedures. The conservative value is the one that loads the knee less, and it is a clinician-set value. All of these are OPINION (level 5 in substance). None rests on graft strain data (`research/citation-audit.md` section 4.1).

Ask your surgeon:
- Which exercises and knee angles are allowed for my graft and my surgery type.
- Whether I had a combined procedure. If yes, an isolated MPFLR rule does not apply and a surgeon protocol is required.

Gaps:
- Graft strain during exercise after MPFLR: NONE. The literature gives joint stress in healthy people (S14, S15, S17, S18, N23) and protocol opinion (P5, P7).
- The angle bands of S15 (45 to 90 degrees) and S17 (10 to 40 degrees) are UNVERIFIED (C7, C8).
- The searches covered the sources up to 2026-11-08. A paywalled or later study can exist. This answer says only that the knowledge base has none.
