# Does N31 show better knee extension with tele-rehab?

PICO: The question does not say the surgery type. The answer covers people after knee surgery in general, not MPFL reconstruction. N31 is not an MPFLR study. Weeks since surgery are not stated. Question: does tele-rehab give better knee extension than usual care?

## Answer

No. N31 does not support a claim of better knee extension with tele-rehab. The source is not consistent with itself about what the 9.64 degree value measures. Do not use it as a benefit of tele-rehab.

## What these facts mean

- The abstract says "knee extension range". The results text says "active extension lag". These are two different measures. The source gives both names to one value.
- The value is MD = 9.64 degrees with k = 3 trials. Three trials is a small base. The 95% CI is 6.89 to 12.39 and P = .049, which is close to the usual limit.
- The prediction interval is PI = 0.60 to 18.68. It is wide. A new trial can show a value near zero.
- The sign and direction of a lag difference are not explained in the source text that the knowledge base holds. I do not state them.
- The earlier summary in `clinical-update.md` section 2.3 said "Better KOOS (SMD 0.58) and knee extension (MD 9.64 degrees)". Correction C12 in `research/source-audit.md` replaces it. The correction wins.

## Disagreements

- The abstract of N31 says "knee extension range". The results text of N31 says "active extension lag". No value is conservative to pick, because both name the same number. Do not average them. Do not choose one name. Report that the source differs from itself.

## Population and fit

- N31 covers mixed knee surgery. No MPFLR study of tele-rehab exists. Every N31 number is BORROWED.
- No trial of camera feedback after MPFLR exists. This is NONE.
- Any value that comes from N31 is a reference for a clinician. It cannot support an app rule.
- The N31 date is 2025, so it is not older than 5 years.

## Ask your surgeon

- Ask your surgeon or physiotherapist if tele-rehab is an option after your surgery.
- Ask who sets your knee extension goals. A surgeon protocol sets them, not an app.

## Gaps

- NONE: a trial of tele-rehab after isolated MPFLR. What the literature says instead: N31 (mixed knee surgery, 19 RCTs) and N32 (internet-based programmes, 14 studies, 1019 ACLR, ROM and adherence improved at 6 to 12 weeks, heterogeneous). Both are BORROWED.
- NONE: a clean, consistent measure of knee extension with tele-rehab in N31. The source differs from itself (C12).
- NONE: a trial that shows an algorithm that adjusts the plan helps. Phase change stays a surgeon decision.
