PICO: Isolated MPFL reconstruction (no bone or cartilage procedure). Age group: P2 covers ages 8 to 25, P1 states no age limit. Time: after the first phase, from about week 7 on. Question: which open-chain knee extension arc do the guidelines allow later?

Answer: No guideline gives one arc. P2 says 90 to 30 degrees. P1 says 90 to 40 degrees. A study of ACL patients says 90 to 45 degrees. All are OPINION in substance. The surgeon sets the arc.

Disagreements:
- The three arcs differ in the end angle: 30, 40 and 45 degrees. Show all three. None is a consensus.
- P3 (NHS Lothian) allows open-chain quadriceps work from 2 weeks with no arc: "OKC quadriceps exercises should be progressed as able". So the restriction is in 2 of 4 readable guidance sources. It is not a general agreement.
- N3 (2024, PMC11180699) restricts open chain only after bone procedures.
- S1 uses "both open and closed chain" in the strengthening phase.
- The most conservative value is the arc that stops at the larger angle, 90 to 40 or 90 to 45 degrees with surgeon clearance. P1 also says to follow the most conservative guideline.
- P1 keeps open-chain extension restricted through phase 2 (weeks 7 to 11) unless the surgeon clears it. So the restriction lasts longer than phase 1.

Ask your surgeon:
- Is open-chain knee extension allowed for me now, and in which arc?
- When can the arc change?
- Does my surgery include a bone or cartilage procedure? If it does, none of these values apply. A surgeon protocol is required.

Gaps:
- NONE: no study measures graft strain or patellofemoral stress during these arcs after MPFLR. Graft statements are OPINION.
- NONE: no trial compares open-chain arcs after MPFLR. The literature gives only stress data from healthy people (S14 to S18, N23). These are BORROWED.
- NONE: no week, no repetition count and no load for the later arc. The surgeon sets them.
- Not usable: BASK 2021 (G1), AWMF S2e 012-024 and the primary text of N1 were NOT READABLE in the audit.
- The first-phase rule in the app (seated hold "Straighten your knee") conflicts with P2 rec 29 and P1 phase 1. This is a design finding and not a patient value.

Use in the app: the arc must be a field that the clinician sets. The values above are reference ranges for the clinician only. The app never changes the phase and never chooses an arc for a person.
