There is no single arc that the guidelines agree on. The sources give different arcs, and all of them are low-level evidence (level 5, expert opinion).

Knee flexion arcs for later open-chain work (the knee bends to 90 degrees and extends to a limit):

| Source | Arc | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| P2 (MPFL guideline, rec 39) | 90 to 30 degrees | Given "initially only". It is in a Note that says "It may be helpful", so it has a lower weight. P2 rec 29 avoids 30 to 0 degrees in the initial phase. |
| P1 (HSS MPFLR guideline) | 90 to 40 degrees | A "limited arc of motion" for open chain strengthening. It comes under surgeon preference. P1 forbids active open-chain extension in weeks 0 to 6 and, in weeks 7 to 11, allows it only if the surgeon clears it. |
| AS1 (ACL source, BORROWED) | 90 to 45 degrees | From week 4. This is an ACL source, not an MPFL source. Do not treat it as MPFL evidence. |

A healthy-subject lab study (N23, BORROWED) also used 90 to 45 degrees for knee extension.

Points to keep in mind:
- Two guidelines (P2, P1) support an arc of about 90 to 30 or 90 to 40 degrees. Do not present one arc as the consensus.
- P3 (NHS Lothian) allows open-chain quadriceps work from 2 weeks with no arc. Another source (N3) restricts open chain only after bony procedures. So even the restriction is in only 2 of 4 readable guidance sources.
- The arc is safe for the app only as a value that the surgeon or clinician sets, with surgeon clearance. The app must not set it by itself.
- In the first phase (weeks 0 to 11 in the app design), no seated extension to 0 degrees. Avoid the terminal 30 to 0 degrees early.

Summary: later, a mid-range arc of about 90 to 30, 90 to 40 or 90 to 45 degrees, depending on the source. The surgeon must choose the arc and clear it. The evidence is expert opinion only.
