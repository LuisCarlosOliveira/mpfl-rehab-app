PICO: Isolated MPFL reconstruction (the case that this answer covers), adolescent and adult, about 3 to 9 months after surgery. Question: which hop test limb symmetry index (LSI) threshold do guidelines give?

## Answer

No single threshold exists. The guidelines give 85 percent, 90 percent or 95 percent. The conservative value is 90 percent or more. Do not average the values. No validated return-to-sport battery exists after MPFLR (S1, N4, S6). Almost all of these values are OPINION in substance.

Disagreement: the values run from 85 to 95 percent. The conservative value is 90 percent or more for strength and hops (S1, ME1). ME1 also says that "no clear criteria can be as yet endorsed".

## Context that limits use

- Few patients meet the criteria. S6: "only 14% to 40% of patients met all study-specific RTS criteria". S6 is a mixed population (41 percent had a bone procedure, C9).
- N20 (case series, 28 adolescents, 10 had TTO): quadriceps LSI "85.3%", "Only 32.0% of patients passed all 4 hop tests" at a mean 7.4 months.
- No MPFLR study links passing a hop test to re-injury (S6).
- LSI is "inappropriate" in bilateral instability (N3, OPINION).
- Combined surgery (TTO, trochleoplasty, cartilage procedure): a surgeon protocol is required. Do not apply these values.
- The app cannot measure hop tests. A webcam comparison is "movement symmetry", never "LSI". The threshold is a clinician input, not an app limit.

## Ask your surgeon

- Which hop tests and which LSI value apply to my case.
- When hop testing starts. P3 asks for 90 percent strength first. S1 gives hop tests "starting at 12-19 weeks".
- Whether my surgery was combined, and whether LSI applies if both knees were operated.

## Gaps

- NONE: no validated MPFLR hop LSI threshold. The literature gives a borrowed ACL value of 90 percent or more (S1).
- UNVERIFIED: the BASK 2021 consensus (G1) is paywalled. Its rehab content is not in the knowledge base.
