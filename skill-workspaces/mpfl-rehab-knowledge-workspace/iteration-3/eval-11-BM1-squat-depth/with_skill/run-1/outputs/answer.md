# Squat depth and patellofemoral stress

PICO: Isolated MPFL reconstruction, age group not stated (P2 covers ages 8 to 25, P1 and P3 state no age limit), weeks since surgery not stated, question: which squat depth limits patellofemoral stress. This answer covers a closed-chain squat in the strengthening phase of isolated MPFLR.

## Answer

No source gives a proven safe squat depth after MPFL reconstruction. The surgeon sets the depth.

The only direct evidence on depth and patellofemoral stress comes from healthy people. It is BORROWED. It points to a shallow range, 0 to 50 degrees of knee flexion, as the range that limits stress. Stress rises as the knee bends. It is highest near 90 degrees.

No study measures graft strain or patellofemoral stress during a squat after MPFLR. For a graft strain number, the answer is NONE.

The app must not choose a depth for a patient. A depth is a protocol field that a clinician sets. The values below are reference ranges for the clinician.

## Disagreements

- Depth values differ by source: 0 to 50 degrees (S16, N3, healthy or early protocol), 45 to 0 degrees (N23, healthy), 0 to 75 degrees (P8), no more than 90 degrees (P5), 90 degrees as a goal (P2).
- The conservative value is 0 to 50 degrees. I do not average the values.
- The ranges are not equal in kind. S16 and N23 are lab studies on healthy people. P2, P5 and P8 are protocols or goals. None is outcome evidence after MPFLR.
- Another gap: S18 gives 13.8 MPa (step-down) and 11.5 MPa (lateral step-up). The value 11.2 MPa for the forward step-up is UNVERIFIED (C6). This is not a squat value.

## Ask your surgeon

- How deep can I bend my knee in a squat at my stage?
- Did my surgery include a bone or cartilage procedure? Then these values do not apply, and I need your protocol. (Combined surgery, for example tibial tubercle osteotomy, needs a surgeon protocol. Never apply an isolated MPFLR rule to it.)
- Which exercises and which weight can I add, and when?

## Gaps

- Safe squat depth after MPFLR: NONE. The literature gives only lab data from healthy people (S14 to S16, N23).
- Graft strain by knee angle during a squat after MPFLR: NONE. Graft statements are OPINION.
- Knee valgus threshold during a squat: NONE. S1 (level 5) says to monitor femoral internal rotation with dynamic knee valgus: "Closed-chain exercises should be closely monitored to prevent femoral internal rotation coupled with dynamic knee valgus". This is OPINION and has no number.
- Camera note: a 2D side-view knee angle has an error of about 9 to 10 degrees as a working margin, from F1 (citation-audit V85). The error depends on the system (M8). An app cannot use a depth limit that is smaller than the error of its own model, and a webcam angle is not a measurement.

This answer is general information from the project knowledge base. It is not advice for one person. The phase, the depth and the load are decisions for your surgeon and physiotherapist.
