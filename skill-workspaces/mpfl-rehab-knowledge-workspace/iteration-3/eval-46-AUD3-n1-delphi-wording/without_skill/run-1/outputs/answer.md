No, not as a rule, and not as a direct quote of N1.

Why:

1. The wording is second-hand. N1 is Hurley ET et al., Bone Joint J 2023;105-B(12):1265-1270 (modified Delphi, 60 surgeons, 11 countries). Only its abstract is readable. The full text is behind a paywall. The sentence "full ROM should be achieved by 6 weeks after MPFL repair or reconstruction" comes from S10 (Vidra 2026, Curr Rev Musculoskelet Med 19(1):45), which reports the Delphi in its Discussion. The knowledge base marks it "N1 via S10", V-second-hand. S10 paraphrases it, and the primary text was not read (citation-audit V9 and V91; clinical-update; search-log A10 and Q6).

2. What N1 itself says on rehab, in the readable abstract, is the opposite of a firm rule: "There was also no consensus on postoperative immobilization or weightbearing". Only 19 of 41 statements reached strong consensus. The app must not present N1 as a source of firm rehab rules.

3. The strength is limited. The citation audit rates "N1 via S10" as CONSENSUS of 60 surgeons with unread wording. It supports early motion as a principle only. It is expert opinion (LoE 5), not a guideline and not a validated protocol.

4. The 6-week figure is not uniform in the other sources. P2 gives 120 degrees or more by 6 weeks. S1 says full ROM and weight-bearing at 6 weeks, but also "full knee flexion between 4 to 12 weeks". In S10 Table 2 (35 isolated MPFLR protocols), full ROM came by 0 to 6 weeks in 37 percent, by 6 to 12 weeks in 51 percent, and after 12 weeks in 6 percent. R2 reports full ROM by week 6 in 48.4 percent of protocols.

What the app can say:

- Cite it as an expectation, with the real source chain: "A Delphi of 60 surgeons (Hurley 2023), as reported in Vidra 2026, recommended full range of motion by about 6 weeks after MPFL repair or reconstruction. I could not read the original statement wording."
- Or cite it as "N1 via S10", REVIEW or CONSENSUS (second-hand), at the level of the early-motion principle.
- The app must not use "must", "required" or "rule". It must not show the statement as a quote from N1. It must not use it to block, force or auto-progress a patient.
- Before week 6, flexion limits stay a surgeon decision. The generator adds no ceiling of its own (protocol-generator-design G7). Brace and weight-bearing also stay "Ask your surgeon" (G12), because N1 found no consensus.
- If the user needs the exact N1 wording, someone must read the Bone Joint J full text. Until then it stays labelled second-hand.
