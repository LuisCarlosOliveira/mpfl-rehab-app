PICO: isolated MPFL reconstruction (ISO), age group not stated (the N1 panel is mixed surgeons, not an age group), timing: weeks 0 to 6 and later, question: can the app cite the N1 Delphi statement "full ROM by 6 weeks" as a rule.

Answer: No. The app can mention the N1 statement only as a consensus view that supports early motion in general. It cannot cite it as a rule or as a deadline.

Reasons:
- The wording is second-hand. Nobody on the project read the N1 primary text. The audit marks the primary text NOT READABLE (paywall, only the abstract is open). A row with that mark is not usable for a number, a quote or an app rule.
- The wording comes from the S10 review, which reports the Delphi. The audit checked that the S10 quote is exact, but S10 is a review that retells N1. The app must cite S10 as the source of the quote, and say that N1 was not opened.
- The word is "should be achieved". It is a consensus statement of a panel. It is not a rule, a limit or a pass or fail point.
- The N1 abstract itself says that there was "no consensus on postoperative immobilization or weightbearing". So the panel did not agree on the whole early protocol.
- Practice does not match a 6 week rule. In S10 Table 2 (35 ISO protocols), full ROM was targeted by 0 to 6 weeks in 12 (37%), by 6 to 12 weeks in 18 (51%) and after 12 weeks in 2 (6%). S1 says "full knee flexion between 4 to 12 weeks" in its text and "at six weeks" in its conclusion. S1 disagrees with itself (correction C2).
- A target for flexion is passive range in P2 (PROM). A camera sees active motion. The app must not compare the two.

Disagreements: the values run from 6 weeks (N1 via S10, S1 conclusion) to 12 weeks (S1 text, S10 Table 2 majority). Show each value with its source. The conservative value is the later one, and the surgeon sets the date. Do not average. The knowledge base has no single value (`rehab-rules.md`: "No single value. Show the protocol value only").

What the app can do:
- Show "Many protocols and one expert panel expect full motion between 6 and 12 weeks. Your surgeon sets your target." Cite S10 and say that the N1 text was not opened.
- Keep the full ROM week as a field that the clinician sets. The knowledge base values are a reference range only.
- Do not use the statement for a deadline, an alert, a goal message or a phase change. The app never changes the phase.
- A persistent miss of motion can lead to "contact your care team", based on P1 ("Contact surgeon if ROM not trending towards 90° by 4 weeks") and P2 red flags ("Unexpected loss or minimal progression of ROM"). Both are OPINION in substance (level 5).

Ask your surgeon:
- When must my knee reach full bend and full straight?
- Does my surgery include a bone or cartilage step? If yes, an isolated MPFLR rule does not apply and a surgeon protocol is required.
