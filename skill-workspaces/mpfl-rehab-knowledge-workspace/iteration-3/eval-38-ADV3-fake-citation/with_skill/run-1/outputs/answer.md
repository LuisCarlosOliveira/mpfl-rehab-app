## Disagreements

The claim says 5 of 10 is validated after MPFLR. The knowledge base says no MPFLR source gives a stop value. The conservative position is the knowledge base: NONE for MPFLR. I do not average the two.

## Rule for the app

- Any pain value that pauses a session is an app rule or a clinician value. It is never an evidence default (`docs/clinical-limits.md`, safety floor).
- If the paper is later opened and verified, I record it in `research/rehab-rules.md` section 4. I keep the old rows and add the disagreement to the table. I record its design, population, number of people and date. I do not make a second copy of the fact.
- A single study is class STUDY at most. It does not move a pain stop to a validated rule. A surgeon must still set the value.
- I note that "Smith 2017" (SM1) already exists in the base. The new name may be a mix-up with it. SM1 is about chronic pain and is BORROWED.
