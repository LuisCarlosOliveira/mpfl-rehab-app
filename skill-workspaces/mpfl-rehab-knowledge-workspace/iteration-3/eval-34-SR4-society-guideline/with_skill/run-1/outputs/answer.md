PICO: isolated MPFL reconstruction (MPFLR), age group not stated (adults and adolescents), weeks since surgery not stated. Question: is there a society guideline that the protocol can cite?

## Answer

Yes, one society guideline exists. The app cannot cite it for any rule, because nobody on the project read its full text. The readable guidelines come from institutions, not societies. The audit grades their rehab items as OPINION in substance.

| P1: HSS MPFL reconstruction guideline, revised 8/2024 | Institution guideline. Gives no evidence grades | OPINION | VERIFIED (full PDF text) |
| P3: NHS Lothian, May 2022, review May 2027 | Regional guideline. Says of itself: "this is a guideline based on limited evidence and expert opinion" | OPINION | VERIFIED (full PDF text) |

All four carry population ISO (isolated MPFLR). P2 covers ages 8 to 25 only. For adults, only P1, P3, S1 and N3 apply, and all are OPINION (`research/citation-audit.md` section 6).

## Disagreements

- The three readable guidelines and S1 differ on weeks, on open-chain rules and on flexion limits. See `research/rehab-rules.md` sections 1 and 2 and `research/citation-audit.md` section 5.
- The base has no consensus on weight-bearing or brace. N1 (Hurley 2023 Delphi, via S10): "no consensus on postoperative immobilization or weightbearing". The primary text is NOT READABLE (V91).
- No validated rehab protocol exists. S1: "no validated rehabilitation protocols are established" (V1, EXACT).
- Conservative handling: show the source for each value and let the clinician choose. Never average.

## How to cite in the protocol

- Cite P1, P2 and P3 as "institution guideline, expert opinion" with their dates. Do not write "society guideline".
- Do not cite G1 (BASK 2021) or the AWMF guideline for any number or rule. Both are UNVERIFIED.
- Every number stays a field that a clinician sets. The guideline values are reference ranges only (`research/citation-audit.md` section 6).
- To make G1 usable, a person must open the full text and give the link. Then record the URL, the access mark and the exact quotes in `research/rehab-rules.md`. The evidence auditor agent can do the check.
