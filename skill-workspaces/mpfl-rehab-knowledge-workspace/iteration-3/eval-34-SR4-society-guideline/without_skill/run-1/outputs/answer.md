Short answer: there is no society guideline that the app can cite for its rehab rules. One national society consensus exists, but nobody on the project has read its rehab content. The app must not cite it as the source of a rule.

Correction first. `research/clinical-update.md` section 3.2 says "no society guideline for post-operative MPFLR rehab was found". The later audit (`research/source-audit.md`, correction C17) replaces that statement.

**The closest society document: BASK 2021 (G1)**
- Bailey MEA, Metcalfe A, Hing CB, Eldridge J, BASK Patellofemoral Working Group. Consensus guidelines for management of patellofemoral instability. Knee 2021;29:305-312. https://doi.org/10.1016/j.knee.2021.02.018
- It is a national formal consensus. The British Orthopaedic Association ratified it.
- The abstract says it covers "appropriate post operative physiotherapy".
- The full text is behind a paywall. The project read the abstract only. Its rehab content is UNVERIFIED, so no rule in the protocol generator comes from it.
- You can cite it only as "a consensus exists that covers post-operative physiotherapy". Do not cite it for a number, a phase or a limit. Read the full text first. The design documents list this as an open item.

**Other society and consensus documents, and why they do not fit**
- ESSKA 2024 formal consensus (Balcarek 2025, N2): covers first-time dislocation. It has no post-operative MPFLR rehab statement. It does not support a protocol rule.
- Modified Delphi on patellar instability, part II (Hurley 2023, N1): abstract only. Its rehab statements are second-hand. It found no consensus on post-operative immobilization or weight-bearing.
- ISAKOS Sports Medicine Committee 2013 return-to-play criteria (IS1): read only as Table 1 in Menetrey 2014. The primary document was not found. It is second-hand, and it covers return to play, not the weekly protocol.
- German Trauma Society (DGU) AWMF S2e guideline 012-024, 2021: it exists, but the PDF could not be fetched. UNVERIFIED and not read.
- AOSSM, JOSPT, APTA, AAOS: the searches found no guideline for rehab after MPFL reconstruction. The APTA guideline (Willy 2019) is for patellofemoral pain, not instability.

**What the app can cite instead (institution guidelines, read in full)**
- P1: Hospital for Special Surgery, MPFL reconstruction post-operative guideline, revised 8/2024.
- P2: Cincinnati Children's Evidence-Based Care Guideline 46, January 2015. It grades its items, but almost all are evidence level 5, which means local consensus. It is older than 5 years, so flag the date.
- P3: NHS Lothian, May 2022 (review May 2027). Its content is mostly expert opinion.
- These are institution or regional guidelines, not society guidelines. Label them that way. The project marks most rehab rules from them as CONSENSUS (low) or OPINION.

**Advice**
- State in the app and in any document that no validated protocol and no society-endorsed protocol exists. Say that the surgeon and physiotherapist decide.
- If a society citation is needed, the next step is to get the BASK 2021 full text through the user's own access, then add a row per rule to `research/rehab-rules.md`.
- Give the source date for every guideline you cite.
- I did not search the web for this answer. It rests on the project knowledge base. A newer society guideline since the last search is possible.
