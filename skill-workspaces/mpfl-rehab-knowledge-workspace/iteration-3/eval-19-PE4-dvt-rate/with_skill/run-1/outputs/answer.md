# 30-day DVT rate after isolated MPFLR

PICO: isolated MPFL reconstruction, adults (the registry has no adolescents), first 30 days after surgery. Question: how often does DVT occur.

## Answer

The 30-day DVT rate after isolated MPFLR is 0.3 percent. This is 2 patients out of 617. One registry gives this number. The follow-up is only 30 days. It does not cover later events.

## Limits of this number

- N11 is a registry study. The title is about concomitant tibial tubercle osteotomy (TTO). The isolated MPFLR group is a subgroup.
- The rate has no confidence interval in the knowledge base. With 2 events, the true rate is uncertain.
- Prophylaxis use is unknown in this registry.
- Do not apply this rate to combined surgery (TTO, trochleoplasty, cartilage procedure). A surgeon protocol is required for those.

## Disagreements

No source in the knowledge base gives a different 30-day DVT rate after MPFLR. No conservative value to choose.

## Ask your surgeon

- What is my own risk of DVT, given my health and the surgery type?
- Do I need any blood-clot prevention? (The knowledge base gives no advice on this.)
- Which signs of DVT must I report, and who do I call?

## Gaps

- Rate beyond 30 days: NONE. No MPFLR source gives it.
- Rate in adolescents: NONE. N11 has no adolescents.
- Prophylaxis after MPFLR: NONE. No MPFLR source covers it. Borrowed data: a Cochrane review of knee arthroscopy (N12, Perrotta 2022, V-abstract, 8 studies, 3818 adults, BORROWED) found that low-molecular-weight heparin made little or no difference to PE (RR 1.81, moderate certainty) or symptomatic DVT (RR 0.61, low certainty). This is BORROWED from another surgery. It is not advice for MPFLR.
- Earlier text in `research/clinical.md` section 7 that says "no MPFLR VTE study" is out of date. `clinical-update.md` corrects it with N11.

If you have a calf pain, swelling, warmth or redness now, contact your care team now. For chest pain or sudden breathlessness, call emergency services.
