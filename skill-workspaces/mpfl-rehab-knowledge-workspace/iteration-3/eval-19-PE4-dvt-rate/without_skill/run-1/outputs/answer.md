The 30-day DVT rate after isolated MPFLR is 0.3 percent: 2 of 617 patients.

Source: Agarwalla A et al., Orthop J Sports Med 2019 (https://doi.org/10.1177/2325967119837639). Table 2, MPFLR column. The same table gives these 30-day rates for the same 617 patients:
- Pulmonary embolism: 2 patients (0.3 percent).
- Surgical site infection: 8 patients (1.3 percent).
- Any adverse event: 18 patients (2.9 percent).

Limits:
- The data come from one registry (NSQIP). The study title is about concomitant tibial tubercle osteotomy. The isolated MPFLR group is a subgroup.
- The registry holds no adolescents.
- Follow-up is only 30 days. Later events are not counted.
- The rate is low, but it is one source. The data do not show whether patients received thromboprophylaxis.

This number is a rate and nothing more. It is not a recommendation on prophylaxis, and it does not diagnose anything. No source in the project covers anticoagulation after MPFLR.
