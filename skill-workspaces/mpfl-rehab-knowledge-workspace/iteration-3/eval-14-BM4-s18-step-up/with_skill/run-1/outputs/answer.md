**PICO:** Not stated in the question. This answer covers healthy adults (S18 is a lab study, N=20). It is not about MPFLR patients. Weeks since surgery: not applicable.

**Answer:** The knowledge base has an 11.2 MPa value for the forward step-up from S18, but this value is UNVERIFIED. Do not use it as a fact or in an app rule. The S18 abstract gives only two values: step-down and lateral step-up. The forward step-up value is not in the abstract, and the full text is paywalled and was not read.

| Value | Exact quote | Source | Class | Access | Population |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Forward step-down (FSD): 13.8 MPa | "FSD (mean ± SD, 13.8 ± 0.4 MPa) compared to the LSU (11.5 ± 0.8 MPa" | S18, Chinkulprasert et al., JOSPT 2011;41(4):241-248 (`research/source-audit.md`, S18 row) | STUDY | VERIFIED (abstract only) | Healthy adults, BORROWED |
| Lateral step-up (LSU): 11.5 MPa | Same quote as above | S18 (`research/source-audit.md`, correction C6) | STUDY | VERIFIED (abstract only) | Healthy adults, BORROWED |
| Forward step-up: 11.2 MPa | None. It is not in the abstract | S18, as listed in `research/clinical.md` section 4 | STUDY | UNVERIFIED (correction C6) | Healthy adults, BORROWED |

**Disagreements:** None between sources. `research/clinical.md` section 4 states 11.2 MPa for the forward step-up. Correction C6 in `research/source-audit.md` wins over the older file. It marks 11.2 UNVERIFIED. The two values that the abstract gives (13.8 and 11.5 MPa) are the conservative base. The abstract states that step-down gives more stress than lateral step-up.

**Other S18 details:**
- The comparison is at 45 degrees of knee flexion (`research/clinical.md` section 4). I did not check this detail against the abstract.
- S18 is from 2011, which is older than 5 years.
- The S18 values are patellofemoral joint stress in healthy people. They are BORROWED. No study measures MPFL graft strain during exercise after MPFLR. For a graft strain number: NONE.

**Ask your surgeon:** Whether and when step-ups and step-downs are allowed for you, and at what step height. The app does not choose this.

**Gaps:**
- Forward step-up stress value: NONE that is verified. The literature (S18 abstract) gives step-down 13.8 MPa and lateral step-up 11.5 MPa only.
- Graft strain in step-up after MPFLR: NONE.
- Any limit for step-up in MPFLR protocols: not in the knowledge base. The step-up appears by name only, in the phase 2 list of P3 ("mini squats, leg press, heel raises, step ups"). That is OPINION, from guidance in `research/rehab-rules.md`.
- The row "Step-up: about 45 degrees at the top step (S18)" in `research/clinical.md` rests on the unverified full text. Do not use it as an app rule.
