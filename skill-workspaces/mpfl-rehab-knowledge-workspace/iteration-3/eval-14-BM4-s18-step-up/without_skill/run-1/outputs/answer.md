S18 gives no verified number for the forward step-up.

S18 is Chinkulprasert C et al., J Orthop Sports Phys Ther 2011;41(4):241-248 (a lab study of 20 healthy adults, step-up and step-down).

What the abstract gives, at 45 degrees of knee flexion:
- Forward step-down (FSD): 13.8 +/- 0.4 MPa patellofemoral stress.
- Lateral step-up (LSU): 11.5 +/- 0.8 MPa.

The forward step-up value of 11.2 MPa appears in `research/clinical.md` (section 4). It is not in the abstract. The full text is paywalled, so nobody checked it. The source audit (`research/source-audit.md`, correction C6) marks 11.2 MPa as UNVERIFIED. Do not quote it as a verified finding.

What is safe to say:
- Step-down gave more stress than lateral step-up in S18 (13.8 vs 11.5 MPa).
- The forward step-up was probably lower than step-down, and `clinical.md` uses it that way in the exercise table ("same step height gives more stress than step-down"). Treat this as unconfirmed support, not as a measured result.
- All S18 data come from healthy people. The link to MPFL graft safety is UNVERIFIED.
