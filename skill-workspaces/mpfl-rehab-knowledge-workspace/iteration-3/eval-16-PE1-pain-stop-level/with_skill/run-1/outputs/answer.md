# Pain level that stops an exercise after MPFLR

**PICO:** Isolated MPFLR (no bone or cartilage procedure), age group not stated, weeks since surgery not stated. Question: which pain level stops an exercise. This answer covers isolated MPFLR only. After combined surgery (for example TTO), a surgeon protocol is required. Do not apply these rules to it.

## Answer

No source in the knowledge base gives a pain level that stops an exercise after MPFLR. The value is NONE.

Two numbers exist, but neither is a stop rule. The 2 of 10 value is a phase goal. The 5 of 10 value is borrowed from other conditions. Your surgeon or physiotherapist must set your stop level.

If you have pain, swelling, heat or giving way in your knee now, contact your care team now. If you have an emergency sign, call emergency services. This answer gives no other advice for a symptom now.

## Disagreements

- 2 of 10 (P2, goal for walking and daily tasks) and 5 of 10 (PM1, borrowed model for activity) are different quantities. They are not two thresholds for the same rule.
- The citation audit (section 4.4) says that 2 of 10 is NOT SUPPORTED as a session stop rule or an exercise pain limit. A stop rule at 2 of 10 would stop sessions too often.
- The 5 of 10 value was never tested after surgery. It must not be a default after MPFLR (`rehab-rules.md` section 4).
- Conservative value: the lower number, 2 of 10, loads the knee less. But it is a goal and not a stop rule. Do not use either one as an evidence-based stop level.

## App rule, not evidence

The app has a safety floor. It shows "pause and contact your care team" when pain is 7 or more, or when swelling is "more than before". A protocol can set a lower value. The value 7 is a PROPOSAL. It is an app rule and not a clinical default. A clinician must review it. Source: `MPFL_APP_PLAN.md` FR12 and `docs/clinical-limits.md` ("The safety floor"). No study supports the value of 7.

## Ask your surgeon

- At what pain level do I pause or stop an exercise?
- At what swelling level do I pause the program?
- Which pain is normal during exercise, and which pain needs a call to you?

These are surgeon decision 14 in `research/clinical.md` section 9 ("Pain and swelling thresholds that pause the program").

## Gaps

- NONE: a numeric stop level for pain or swelling during exercise after MPFLR. The literature gives a phase goal (2 of 10, P2, level 5) and a borrowed model (5 of 10, PM1 and N25, tendinopathy). Neither was tested after MPFLR.
- NONE: a way for the app to grade effusion. The stroke test (N24, kappa 0.75, 75 patients) is a clinician test and BORROWED. The app has patient report only.
- G1 (BASK 2021 consensus) may hold rehab content. It is UNVERIFIED because the full text is paywalled. It cannot support an app rule.
- Evidence age: P2 is from January 2015, so it is older than 5 years. It was not revised.
