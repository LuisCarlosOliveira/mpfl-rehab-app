PICO: Isolated MPFLR is the case that this answer covers. The question names no age group and no week. The question is about a valgus limit for the mini squat.

Answer: No. The knowledge base has no valgus threshold for the mini squat after MPFLR. The value is NONE. The guidance only says to watch for valgus. It gives no angle, no distance and no pass or fail line.

Disagreements: No source gives a valgus value, so no values conflict. The sources differ on feedback. S1 says to monitor valgus. Willy 2019 recommends against visual biofeedback on alignment, but it is BORROWED from patellofemoral pain. Name the conservative reading: the clinician watches valgus, and the app gives no valgus cue.

What the app can do (`research/form-evidence.md` row 7): NO BUILD as a patient cue. The 2D frontal angle is not valid, and the error (about 19 degrees) is larger than any limit that a clinician would set. The app can only LOG a change from the start of a rep, and only after it measures the error of its own `lite` model (task T13). The log is for a clinician. The app must not use a verdict word about a rep. Any threshold that someone proposes is a PROPOSAL and needs clinician review.

Ask your surgeon or physiotherapist:
- Which squat depth do you allow for the mini squat? The surgeon sets the depth.
- How must you check knee alignment during the squat? Ask for a clinician check in person or by a trusted view.
- Does your surgery include a bone or cartilage procedure? If yes, this isolated MPFLR answer does not apply. A surgeon protocol is required.

Gaps:
- NONE: a valgus angle or distance limit for the mini squat after MPFLR. The literature says instead: monitor valgus and femoral internal rotation (S1, OPINION). It gives no number.
- NONE: any study of the effect of valgus control on graft safety or on recurrence after MPFLR.
- NONE: the error of the `lite` model for a frontal knee angle. No study was found (search E1 and E2 in `research/search-log.md`).
