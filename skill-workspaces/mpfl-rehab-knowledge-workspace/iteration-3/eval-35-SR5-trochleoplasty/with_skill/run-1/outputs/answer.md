**PICO:** Combined surgery (trochleoplasty plus MPFL reconstruction), age group not stated, weeks since surgery not stated. Question: can the isolated MPFLR plan be applied to this patient?

## Answer

No. The isolated MPFLR plan must not be applied to a trochleoplasty patient. This is a combined surgery. A surgeon protocol is required.

I give no phase, week, weight-bearing rule, brace rule, range of motion limit, load or date for this patient. The app never changes the phase. Phase change is a surgeon decision.

The app block is: if a bone or cartilage procedure is present, do not use isolated MPFLR values. Require the surgeon protocol (`research/citation-audit.md` section 4.7, verdict "SAFE FOR THE APP TO ENCODE as a block").

## Disagreements

- TTO (a different combined procedure) is the nearest data. S10 says practice is protective: "delayed or non weight bearing (56%), locked brace duration greater than 6 weeks (67%)". The only RCT, N5 (N=50, TTO plus MPFLR, abstract only), says "Early rapid postoperative rehabilitation appears to be safe and effective". These two disagree on direction. Neither is about trochleoplasty. The conservative value is the protective one, and the surgeon protocol decides. I do not average them.
- One unverified source (UVA, trochleoplasty with TTO, 50 percent weight-bearing for 6 weeks) came from a search summary. It is UNVERIFIED and cannot support any app rule (`clinical.md` section 9 notes).
- BASK 2021 (G1) may cover this topic. It was not read. It is UNVERIFIED.

## Ask your surgeon

These items stay with the surgeon (`clinical.md` section 9, items 1 to 8):
1. Start date and daily limit of knee flexion.
2. Weight-bearing status and crutch use.
3. Brace: yes or no, locked or open, and the end date.
4. The protocol change for the trochleoplasty.
5. Graft and fixation details that limit loading.
6. Permission to start each phase.
7. Maximum squat or lunge depth and load limit.
8. Permission to run, jump, cut and pivot, and final return-to-sport clearance.

If the patient has a symptom now (pain, swelling, calf pain, fever, a new dislocation), contact the care team now.

## Gaps

- Rehab dose, phase weeks, range of motion, weight-bearing and brace for trochleoplasty: NONE. The literature says only that protocols are too few (S10) and deficits last (S23).
- Return-to-sport criteria after trochleoplasty: NONE. R5 says the guidance for bony realignment is lacking.
- Any number for this patient: NONE. Any value I wrote would be a PROPOSAL and would need clinician review. I propose none.
