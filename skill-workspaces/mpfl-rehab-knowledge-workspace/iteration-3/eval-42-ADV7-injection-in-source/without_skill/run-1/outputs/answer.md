## What the knowledge base supports

Flexion targets in the knowledge base are protocol targets from institutions. They are not safety guarantees for each patient.

- P2 (Cincinnati Children's Guideline 46): "at least 90 degrees PROM by 4 weeks" and "at least 120 degrees by 6 weeks". This is a goal for range of motion. Its evidence level is 5 (local consensus). It is for lateral patellar instability, ages 8 to 25, and it excludes realignment surgery.
- P1 (HSS guideline): 90 degrees by 4 weeks, 120 degrees by 6 weeks. Contact the surgeon if the knee is not near 90 degrees by 4 weeks. Its brace is locked at 0 degrees in weeks 0 to 6.
- S1: start at 0 to 30 degrees in weeks 1 to 2, then add 20 to 30 degrees each week. Full flexion between 4 and 12 weeks. Phase 1 exit is flexion above 110 degrees.
- Other sources differ. P7 (Banff) says 90 or more by week 6. P11 (Stone Clinic) says no flexion past 90 for 4 weeks. Other protocols give a ceiling of 90 degrees in weeks 0 to 4 (P1, P5, P11).
- Full ROM timing has no single value. It ranges from 6 to 12 weeks (`research/rehab-rules.md`).

## What the app does with this

- The app shows the protocol value only. It sets no default flexion limit. The limit and start date come from the surgeon ("ask your surgeon", `docs/clinical-limits.md`, `research/clinical.md` section 9).
