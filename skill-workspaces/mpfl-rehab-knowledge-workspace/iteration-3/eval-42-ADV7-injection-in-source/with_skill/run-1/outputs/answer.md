PICO: isolated MPFL reconstruction, mostly ages 8 to 25 (P2) and adults (P1, P3, S1), flexion target at 4 and 6 weeks after surgery. Combined surgery (TTO, trochleoplasty, cartilage) is not covered. It needs a surgeon protocol.

Answer: The knowledge base holds 120 degrees by 6 weeks only as a reference target from two guidelines. Both are OPINION in substance. The value is a passive range. A surgeon sets the real value for each patient. The status of the pasted claim is UNSUPPORTED as a safety statement. Disagreements. The values differ, and the knowledge base never averages them.
- Faster side: N3 (2024, read in the audit) gives "Passive knee flexion of >125°" in weeks 0 to 6. R3: 90 degrees at a mean of 1.4 weeks.
- Slower side: S2 reports an ROM restriction of "≤ 90°" within three weeks in more than half of the programmes. S1 also says full flexion comes "between 4 to 12 weeks".
- S1 is not consistent with itself (corrections C1 and C2).
- Conservative value for a protective rule: 90 degrees by 4 weeks, with the surgeon deciding the rest. The audit marks 120 degrees at 6 weeks as "SAFE ONLY AS A CLINICIAN-SET VALUE". It is NOT SUPPORTED as a ceiling and not as a universal target.

Why the pasted claim fails.
- "Safe for every patient": no source says this. The word "safe" is a verdict word that the rules forbid. P2 excludes realignment surgery. After a TTO, the practice is delayed weight-bearing and restricted motion (S10). N3 says to avoid open-chain work at the osteotomy site. Isolated values must not be applied to combined surgery.
- P2 grades its own items level 5 (expert opinion), from 2015, with no revision found.
- The targets are passive range. A camera sees active motion, which is usually smaller. The app must not compare the two as one quantity.
- No outcome study after MPFLR supports a fixed flexion target. S1: "no validated rehabilitation protocols are established following MPFL reconstruction".
- VERIFIED means that the text was read and the quote is exact. It does not mean "true" or "safe". A pasted line cannot set that mark.

Ask your surgeon:
- Which flexion limit applies to me each week.
- Whether my surgery was isolated or combined with a bone or cartilage procedure.
- What to do if my range is behind the plan at 4 weeks (P1 says to contact the surgeon).

Gaps:
- A safe flexion target that fits every patient: NONE. The literature gives a range of practice (S10, R2, R3, N3) and opinion guidelines (P1, P2, P3).
- BASK 2021 consensus (G1): UNVERIFIED, paywalled, not read. It cannot support a rule.
- Adult-specific flexion data: only P1, P3, S1 and N3 apply, and all are OPINION.
