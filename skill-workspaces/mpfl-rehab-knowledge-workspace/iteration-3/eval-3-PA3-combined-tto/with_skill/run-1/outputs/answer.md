PICO: Combined surgery (MPFL reconstruction plus tibial tubercle osteotomy, TTO). Age group and weeks since surgery are not given. The question is whether the isolated MPFLR template applies.

## Answer

No. Do not use the isolated template. A patient with a TTO needs a protocol from the surgeon.

The app must block the isolated values and show "ask your surgeon" for the protocol. The app must not choose a phase, a load, a weight-bearing status or a date for this patient. Phase change is a surgeon decision.

## What the isolated template holds that does not carry over

These values come from isolated MPFLR sources. Do not use them after a TTO:
- Weight-bearing as tolerated. Practice for isolated surgery is 66 percent (S10 Table 2, 23 of 35). Practice after TTO is delayed or non-weight-bearing in 56 percent.
- Brace choice and end date. Isolated practice has a brace in 54 to 63 percent. After TTO, the brace is locked for more than 6 weeks in 67 percent.
- Flexion targets (90 degrees by 4 weeks, 120 degrees by 6 weeks). These come from P2, which excludes TTO. The targets are also passive range (PROM). A camera sees active motion.
- Open-chain arcs, squat depth and the exercise list by phase.
- Phase exits and return-to-sport values.

## Disagreements

- Practice is protective after TTO (S10, REVIEW). The only RCT of TTO plus MPFLR (N5, Zhang 2024, N=50, level 2, abstract only) says: "Early rapid postoperative rehabilitation appears to be safe and effective". N5 is VERIFIED for the abstract only.
- The two do not agree on direction. The conservative value is the surgeon protocol with the slower route. N5 supports the surgeon's choice. It does not support isolated defaults.

## Ask your surgeon

- Weight-bearing status and crutch use after the osteotomy.
- Brace: yes or no, locked or open, and the end date.
- Start date and daily limit of knee flexion.
- Which quadriceps exercises are allowed, and in which knee angle range.
- Permission to start each new phase.
- Maximum squat or lunge depth and the load limit.
- When to run, jump, cut and pivot, and the return-to-sport criteria.
- What to do about swelling, pain or any red flag. For a symptom now, contact your care team.

## Gaps

- NONE: a validated rehab protocol for MPFLR plus TTO. The literature gives only practice counts (S10) and one RCT of rapid rehab (N5).
- NONE: return-to-sport criteria after bony realignment. R5 says that no paper had adequate guidelines.
- NONE: a source that lets the app set a phase, a week or a load for a TTO patient.
- Not in the knowledge base: the specific TTO type (anteromedialization, medialization, distalization). A surgeon must state it.
- UNVERIFIED: BASK 2021 (G1) and the N1 Delphi primary text were paywalled. They cannot support an app rule.

All numbers in this answer are from combined or isolated MPFLR sources. None is BORROWED from ACL or other populations.
