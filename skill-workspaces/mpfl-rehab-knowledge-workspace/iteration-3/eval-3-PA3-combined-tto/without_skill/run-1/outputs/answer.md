No. Do not use the isolated template for a patient who also had a tibial tubercle osteotomy (TTO). This patient needs the protocol of the surgeon.

Why:

- The isolated rules do not cover TTO. The 2015 guideline (P2) excludes "tibial tubercle re-alignment" from its scope. The isolated MPFLR values in the knowledge base come from isolated-surgery sources.
- Practice for TTO is more protective. The S10 review of protocols reports that TTO protocols recommended "delayed or non weight bearing (56%)" and "locked brace duration greater than 6 weeks (67%)". For isolated MPFLR, 66 percent of 35 protocols allow immediate weight-bearing as tolerated. S2 reports week-1 immobilisation in 93.8 percent of programmes with TTO and 65.3 percent without. S1 says weight-bearing can be limited after TTO.
- The evidence disagrees on direction. The only randomized trial of rehab speed (N5, 50 patients, level 2, abstract only) was in TTO plus MPFLR. It found that early rapid rehab "appears to be safe and effective", with faster recovery to 12 months and no difference at 24 months. So practice says go slower, and the one trial says faster is safe. No source gives a dose or schedule for TTO that the app can use. Do not pick a side.
- The risk is real. If the app applies an isolated protocol to combined surgery, the patient can be overloaded. S23 (trochleoplasty plus MPFLR, a different procedure) shows lasting deficits at 2 years. It does not cover TTO, but it supports the same caution.

What to do in the app:

- Do not present the isolated template as valid for this patient. Do not set a default for weight-bearing, brace or depth limit and call it consensus. Even for isolated surgery, there is no consensus on these.
- Record the TTO in the protocol field `surgeon.concurrentProcedures` ("Other procedures at the same surgery").
- Load a protocol that the surgeon or physiotherapist wrote for this combined surgery. The surgeon fields (`brace`, `weightBearing`, `depthLimit`, `phaseClearance`) must come from the surgeon. If they are missing, the app shows "Ask your surgeon".
- The generator design lists combined surgery as a non-goal. The app does not approve or validate any plan.

Limits of this answer: N5 is known from its abstract only. The UVA MPFL plus TTO protocol was not readable (the page returned 404), so it is unverified. This answer is not medical advice. The patient follows the care team.
