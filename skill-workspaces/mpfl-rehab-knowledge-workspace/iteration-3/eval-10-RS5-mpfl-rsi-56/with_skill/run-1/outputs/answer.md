PICO: Isolated MPFL reconstruction (MPFLR), age group not stated (the sources cover adults and adolescents), return-to-sport stage (S1 puts it after week 20). Question: is an MPFL-RSI score above 56 a validated cutoff?

## Answer

No. The knowledge base has no source that validates an MPFL-RSI cutoff of 56 for MPFLR. The value is a proposal in one review (S1). It is not a validated threshold. Do not write "validated" for it.

## Disagreements

The cutoffs differ: 56 (S1, MPFL-RSI), 59.2 (S21, ACL-RSI, AUC 0.70), more than 75 (P9, ACL-RSI). They use different scales, groups and aims. Do not average them. The conservative value is the highest, more than 75, but it is UNVERIFIED and cannot support an app rule.

The general return-to-sport picture is also weak: S1, N4 and S6 state that no validated return-to-sport battery exists after MPFLR. ME1 says "no clear criteria can be as yet endorsed".

## Ask your surgeon

- Does my surgeon or therapist use a questionnaire (MPFL-RSI, ACL-RSI or another) to judge readiness to return to sport?
- What decides my return to sport: time, strength tests, hop tests, or my own confidence?

## Gaps

- NONE: no validated MPFL-RSI cutoff for MPFLR. The literature gives instead an unvalidated proposal (S1, from N14) and an ACL-RSI cutoff of 59.2 with weak discrimination (S21, AUC 0.70).
- NONE: no predictive validity of the MPFL-RSI. N15 states none for the ACL-RSI.
- The app must not use 56 as a pass gate or as a "ready" message. If the app shows the score, a clinician sets the number as a protocol field and reviews it. Any value the app suggests is a PROPOSAL.
- N14 is read by abstract only. The full text was not read.
