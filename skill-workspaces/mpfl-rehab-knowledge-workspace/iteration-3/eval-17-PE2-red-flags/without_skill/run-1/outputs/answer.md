# Which signs must go to the surgeon?

Short answer: stop exercising and contact your surgeon or care team if you have any sign below. If you have a sign from the emergency group, call emergency services first. The app does not diagnose. It tells you to stop and to get help.

Important: this list is general guidance. It is UNVERIFIED for MPFL surgery. No MPFL-specific guideline gives stop rules. A clinician must confirm each item and its group. Your own surgeon's written instructions come first. Where they differ from this list, follow the surgeon.

## Emergency: stop, do not exercise, call emergency services now

- Sudden shortness of breath or chest pain (possible blood clot in the lung).
- Your kneecap moved out of place, or you heard a pop and cannot put weight on the leg.
- Your foot is numb, cold or pale, or you cannot feel a pulse in it.

## Contact your surgeon or care team: stop, do not exercise

- Pain, swelling, warmth or redness in your calf (possible deep vein thrombosis).
- Signs of infection: fever, spreading redness, fluid from the wound, or wound pain that grows.
- Large or growing swelling of the knee after exercise. No numeric threshold exists.
- A new feeling that the knee gives way.
- Your knee catches or locks.
- Your knee bend is getting less, or it is not getting better (possible stiffness).

## App safety rule (a proposal)

The app also pauses a session and shows "pause and contact your care team" when pain is 7 out of 10 or more, or when swelling is "more than before". A protocol can set a lower pain limit. The value 7 is a PROPOSAL that a clinician must review.

## Other questions for the surgeon

The app leaves these to the surgeon and shows "ask your surgeon" when the protocol does not set them: knee bend limit in weeks 0 to 6, weight-bearing and crutches, brace use, permission to start each new phase, squat or lunge depth and load, running, jumping and pivoting, return to sport, blood clot prevention, and special cases (age under 18, both knees, repeat surgery, combined surgery).

## Evidence note

Items come from one consensus guideline (Cincinnati Children's, evidence level 5) and general practice. Redislocation, stiffness and wound problems are known complications in the MPFL studies. One registry reports a 30-day DVT rate of 0.3 percent after isolated MPFL surgery. Follow-up is short.
