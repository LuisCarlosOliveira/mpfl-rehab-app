**PICO:** Isolated MPFL reconstruction (the guideline excludes realignment surgery), ages 8 to 25 for P2, any week after surgery. Question: which signs go to the surgeon. The case for combined surgery is below.

**If you have one of these signs now:** contact your care team now. If you have sudden shortness of breath, chest pain, a kneecap that moved out of place with a pop and no weight-bearing, or a foot that is numb, cold or pale, call emergency services now. This answer gives no other advice about your knee.

## Answer

The only guideline list for "notify the surgeon" after MPFL surgery has five signs. It is P2, rec 4. The app adds more signs from general practice. Those added signs have no MPFL source.

P2 is from January 2015. That is older than 5 years. P2 was not revised that the audit could find. The P2 team states these as "It is recommended", which is weak strength. P2 wrote the list for a therapist at an examination. Read it as a referral trigger, not as a measured limit. Over-referral costs little. Under-referral is the risk (`citation-audit.md` section 4.6).

## Signs that the app list adds

`app/src/lib/redflags.ts` adds signs from outside P2. They are general practice and UNVERIFIED for MPFL surgery. A clinician must confirm each item and its group (`docs/clinical-limits.md`, `research/clinical.md` section 7).

| Sign | Group in the app | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Sudden shortness of breath or chest pain | Emergency | UNVERIFIED, general practice |
| Kneecap moved out of place, or a pop and no weight-bearing | Emergency | UNVERIFIED. Recurrent instability is a known failure: 1.2% (S11), 1.9% (S5), 5.4% (S4). The signs are general practice |
| Foot numb, cold or pale, or no pulse | Emergency | UNVERIFIED |
| Fever, spreading redness, wound fluid, growing wound pain | Care team | UNVERIFIED signs. 30-day infection 8 of 617 (1.3%) in isolated MPFLR (N11, STUDY, VERIFIED full text) |
| A new feeling that the knee gives way | Care team | UNVERIFIED, general practice |

The app does not list "rising irritability" as a patient question. It has no sign that a person can answer yes or no.

## Disagreements

- P2 rec 4 has no infection, nerve, circulation or new-dislocation item. P2 Table 1 names infection and sensation loss only as examination items. The app list is wider. The wider list is the conservative one.
- No source gives a number for swelling or pain that must go to the surgeon (NONE). See Gaps.

## Ask your surgeon

- What must I report to you, and how fast?
- Which signs do you want me to treat as an emergency?
- What knee bend do you expect at 4 and at 6 weeks for me?
- Do I need anything against blood clots? (Surgeon decision 12.)
- Which pain or swelling level do you want me to report? (Surgeon decision 14.)
- My surgery included a bone or cartilage procedure (for example TTO). What is my own list?

## Gaps

- NONE: a numeric pain level or swelling level that stops an exercise or triggers a call, for MPFLR. P2 pain "≤ 2 out of 10" is a phase goal for walking and daily tasks (OPINION). It is not a stop rule. The 5 of 10 pain-monitoring model (PM1, Thomeé) is BORROWED from tendon and patellofemoral pain and was never tested after surgery.
- NONE: any MPFL source for the infection, breathing, nerve, circulation and giving-way signs. The literature gives only rates (N11, S11, S5, S4).
- NONE: a rule for revision MPFLR. P2 includes revision in its group but gives no separate rule.
- UNVERIFIED: BASK 2021 (G1) and the Hurley 2023 Delphi (N1) may hold more on this. The full texts are behind a paywall.
- Combined surgery (TTO, trochleoplasty, cartilage procedure): P2 excludes it. Do not use this list as a full list. A surgeon protocol is required.
