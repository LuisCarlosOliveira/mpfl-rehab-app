No. By default, phase 1 must not include the seated knee extension hold to full extension.

Why:
- The hold asks for active open-chain knee extension to 0 degrees (guide text: "Straighten your knee. Hold it").
- Two guidelines in the knowledge base advise against this early:
  - P2, rec 29 (initial phase): short arc (30 to 0 degrees) and long arc quadriceps exercises in the terminal range of 30 to 0 degrees "are avoided to minimize stress to the reconstructed medial patellofemoral ligament".
  - P1 (Hospital for Special Surgery), phase 1: "Do not perform AROM open chain KE". Phase 2: "unless cleared by surgeon". The restriction lasts through week 11 unless the surgeon clears it.
- The protocol generator design (G9) therefore generates no seated extension hold in weeks 0 to 11. This is a protective default.

What phase 1 can include for full extension:
- Quad sets (isometric at 0 degrees) are allowed from day 0 (S1, P1, P3).
- Restoring full extension early is a goal (S1, P2: 0 degrees knee extension AROM). The goal does not need this open-chain exercise.

When the hold can enter:
- Only as an option from week 7, with surgeon clearance and both angles entered by the person (G9, Option B).
- Later open-chain work uses a mid-range arc, for example 90 to 40 or 90 to 30 degrees, not terminal extension.

Caveats:
- The rule is consensus-level (guidelines, evidence level 5), not trial evidence. Some sources disagree: P3 phase 2 (from 2 weeks) says to progress OKC quadriceps work as able. N3 restricts open chain only after bony procedures.
- No clinician has reviewed this. It is an open item in the plan (rank 2, item 4: the seated extension hold out of phase 1, and the week when it can enter).
- If the surgeon enables it, the surgeon must set it. The app must not decide this alone.
- The code still has the `seated-extension-hold` exercise kind. A missing hold time returns "ask your surgeon". Its default band reads a missing target as full extension (0), which is a proposal that needs clinician confirmation.

Advice: ask your surgeon or physiotherapist. Do not add this hold to phase 1 unless they clear it.
