The generator must not fill in a squat depth ceiling. Every clinical number in a protocol is a field that a clinician sets. The app enforces only protective defaults and referrals. A published value, such as 50 degrees, is at best a PROPOSAL. It needs a VERIFIED source id with an evidence class before it can become an app rule. Even then, a surgeon decision stays a surgeon decision.

If the clinician set no depth number, the plan must not look complete. It shows "ask your surgeon" for the squat depth. It sets no default and no limit.

With no limit, the app has nothing to compare the angle against. So it shows no depth cue and no "depth below your target" message. Every angle on screen still carries the note "estimated angle, not a medical measurement".

The generator can still add the app protective rules. These are the red-flag check, the pain safety floor (FR12), the Stop button and the "knee gave way or hurts" button. Do not label these as clinical defaults.

An incomplete plan is correct here. It tells the user and the clinician that one decision is missing.
