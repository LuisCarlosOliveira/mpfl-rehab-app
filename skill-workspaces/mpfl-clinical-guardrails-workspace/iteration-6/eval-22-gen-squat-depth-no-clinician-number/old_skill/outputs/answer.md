Yes, but only under a narrow rule. Do not do it to make the plan look complete.

The mini squat depth of 50 degrees (N3) is one of three numbers that the generator can add. The others are the dose 2 x 10 (P2) and the seated hold arc end 40 degrees (P1). The number is never a default. The person must confirm it.

Four things must hold:
1. The number is a quote from a published MPFL source.
2. The audit row in `research/citation-audit.md` says EXACT for that quote.
3. A source that disagrees shows on the same screen.
4. A surgeon number can only lower the value.

What the plan shows: the 50 degrees, the source, the grade, the population and the line "Not approved by a clinician". It also shows the "estimated angle, not a medical measurement" note. It shows no pass, goal or "within range" message.

If the audit row is not EXACT, the plan does not show a number. It shows "ask your surgeon".

Other numbers stay clinician fields: knee-bend limit, hold time, tempo, session count and pain stop. The generator must leave them empty.
