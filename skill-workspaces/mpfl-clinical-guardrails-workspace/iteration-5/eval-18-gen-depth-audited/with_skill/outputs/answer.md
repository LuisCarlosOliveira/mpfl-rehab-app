Yes. The generator may put a 50-degree mini squat depth ceiling in a plan only if these conditions hold:

1. The number is an exact quote from a published MPFL source, and the row for N3 in `research/citation-audit.md` says EXACT. If it does not, the answer is no.
2. The rule shows the source, its evidence grade, the population, and the line "Not approved by a clinician".
3. A source that disagrees shows on the same screen.
4. A surgeon value can only lower the 50-degree ceiling, never raise it.
5. The person confirms the plan before it applies.
6. The angle carries "estimated angle, not a medical measurement".
7. The screen avoids banned words such as goal, pass, fail and milestone.

N3 is one of the three numbers the guardrails allow the generator to set. The others are the P2 dose and the P1 seated arc end.
