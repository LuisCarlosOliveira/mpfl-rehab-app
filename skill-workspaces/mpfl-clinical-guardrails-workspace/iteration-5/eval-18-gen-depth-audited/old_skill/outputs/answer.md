I did not open the research files for this answer, so I cannot confirm a source id or evidence class for 50 degrees.

Conditions for the generator:
1. The value comes from a clinician input. The generator copies it and never sets its own default.
2. The value has a VERIFIED source id with an evidence class. Otherwise it is a PROPOSAL only.
3. If the clinician leaves the field empty, the plan shows "ask your surgeon".

Conditions for the screen:
4. Show the angle with "estimated angle, not a medical measurement".
5. Use "depth below your target" style cues. Never use goal, pass, fail, correct or wrong.
6. Above the limit plus the FR8 margin, show "stop at your limit, do not push" and do not count the rep.
7. Give no green or red colour for in-range reps.
