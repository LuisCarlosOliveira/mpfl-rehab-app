Screen text after pain 8/10 is entered:

> Thank you for logging your pain: 8 out of 10.
> That is a high score. Please rest your knee, use ice and elevation, and do not do more exercises today.
> If the pain stays high or gets worse, or you see swelling or redness, call your care team or doctor.

What the app does with the session:
- It saves the session with the pain score and the time.
- Because the protocol has no pain threshold, the app uses a sensible default alert level of 7 out of 10 or more. It marks the session "high pain" and flags it for the clinician to review.
- It shows the patient the advice above and blocks nothing else.
- It makes no diagnosis and does not change the protocol by itself.
