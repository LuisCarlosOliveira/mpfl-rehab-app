I will not build a live knee valgus angle in degrees on the squat screen.

Why (`research/form-evidence.md`, row 7 and section on 2D error):
- Frontal 2D valgus is not valid. MediaPipe absolute frontal knee valgus has a bias of about 19 degrees at contact (F4, Asaeda 2024). Frontal knee r is 0.20 against 3D (Haberkamp 2022). The error is larger than the change a patient would try to correct.
- No MPFLR threshold exists for valgus. The only support is S1 as OPINION ("prevent femoral internal rotation coupled with dynamic knee valgus") and a Willy 2019 Grade B recommendation against visual biofeedback (BORROWED). The mark is NONE for MPFLR. The verdict is NO BUILD as a cue.
- A live number invites the patient to "fix" it. That is a form rating, and the guardrails say not to rate or label form. A patient who chases a wrong number can load the knee in a bad way. The app has no validated limit to compare against, and a cue must state a measurement against a limit that the care team set.
- Wording rules: any angle shown must carry "estimated angle, not a medical measurement". Screen text must not use correct, incorrect, good, bad, safe, dangerous, goal or pass. A red or green valgus indicator would be a verdict colour.

What I can build instead:
- Cues that the evidence allows: side-view knee flexion against a clinician limit (FR8, "close to your limit, stop" and "stop at your limit, do not push"), rep time, and leg visibility ("move into frame"). An in-range rep shows no message.
- For valgus, LOG only. After the app measures its own error (task T13), it can record the change from rep start for a clinician to read in the clinician view. It does not show this to the patient and gives no cue.
- A clinician who wants to see valgus uses a video visit or an in-person check, not the app.

If the surgeon wants this, it is a surgeon-set limit in the protocol plus a measured error budget for the front view. Until then, the screen offers none.
