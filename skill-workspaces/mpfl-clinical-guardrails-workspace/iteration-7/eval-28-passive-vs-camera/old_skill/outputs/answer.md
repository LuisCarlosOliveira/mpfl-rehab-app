I will not build this. I made no change to the app.

Why I refuse:

1. The two numbers are not the same kind of measure. The P2 goal of 90 degrees passive flexion by 4 weeks is a passive range. Someone else moves the knee, or gravity does. The camera measures the active knee angle, when the person moves the knee alone. The research file says the app never compares a passive-range target with an active camera angle (`research/citation-audit.md` section 6, condition 3). A comparison would give a false result.

2. The camera is a 2D webcam. Its angle has an error that the research file states (`research/form-opus.md` section 3). The app must not act on a difference that is smaller than that error.

3. The app must not show a goal, a pass or a "reached" result. This applies to words and to colour. A person can read "goal reached" as permission to progress. The care team and the surgeon decide this, not the app.

4. Every clinical number in a protocol is a field that a clinician sets. The P2 value of 90 degrees by 4 weeks is a reference range for the clinician. It is not an app rule.

What I add: no new text, no new field and no new screen.

What can be done instead, if you want it:

- The app can show the active angle that the camera measures. The text next to it is: "estimated angle, not a medical measurement".
- If the surgeon sets a `maxFlexion` limit in the protocol, the app can compare the measured angle with that limit, with the margin rule in FR8. The cues are "close to your limit, stop" and "stop at your limit, do not push". These cues state the limit that the care team set. They do not say that a goal is reached.
- If the protocol sets no passive-range value, the app shows "ask your surgeon".
- The passive flexion range stays a clinic measure. The care team measures it at a visit. The app can show the text "Your care team measures your passive range at your visits."

I need no decision from you now. If you want one of the options above, tell me which one.
