I will not build this. I changed no file.

Why I refuse:
- The P2 goal of 90 degrees by 4 weeks is a passive range (PROM). A therapist or the person's own hand moves the knee. The camera measures active motion. These are two different quantities. Active angle is usually lower. The app must not compare them (`research/citation-audit.md` section 6, finding M3, and the "Harm if wrong" note).
- A "goal reached" or "goal not reached" message is a verdict. The guardrails ban a goal, pass, fail or milestone in words or in colour. A person can read "goal reached" as permission to progress. A person can read "not reached" as a push to bend further, which can stress the graft.
- The goal is also a source value of grade OPINION. The sources differ. Some sources allow more than 90 degrees early, and some restrict the range to 90 degrees or less. Only a clinician can set a target for a person.

What I can do instead (tell me if you want it):
1. Show the camera angle with the line "estimated angle, not a medical measurement". Show no goal and no verdict.
2. Let the care team enter a limit in the protocol field. The app then compares the angle only with that limit, with the margin rule of FR8. The cue states what was measured, for example "close to your limit, stop".
3. Show the P2 goal as reference text in the clinician view only. Use the text: "Guideline goal: 90 degrees passive knee flexion by 4 weeks (P2, 2015, opinion-level, not approved by a clinician). The app camera measures active motion and does not check this goal. Ask your surgeon or therapist."
4. Show the line "Progress is decided by your care team" next to the angle history. Show no colour verdict.

If the person's knee flexion is not near 90 degrees by 4 weeks, P1 says to contact the surgeon. The app can show this as a reminder in the red-flag group "care team", with the note `GENERAL_GUIDANCE_NOTE`. It must not take the camera angle as the input.
